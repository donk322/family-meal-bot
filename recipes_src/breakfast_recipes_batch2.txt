BREAKFAST RECIPES, BATCH 2 (5 dishes)
All quantities below are for 6 portions, as a reference size. In the bot the quantities will be recalculated for any number of portions.
T = the moment the family sits down to eat.

🐟 GREEK SALMON WITH FETA AND FRESH VEGETABLES — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 40 minutes (oven). Start at T minus 45 min.

YOU NEED
• Salmon fillet: 1080 g (6 pieces of 180 g)
• Olive oil: 90 ml (6 tablespoons)
• Limes: 4 (the juice of 3 = about 90 ml, and 1 for slicing)
• Feta: 225 g
• Red onions: 2 small (about 200 g)
• Tomatoes: 250 g (about 2 medium)
• Cucumber: 100 g
• Pitted black olives: 60 g
• Dill: 5 g
• Garlic: 10 g (2 cloves)
• Dried oregano: 1 1/2 teaspoons
• Chili flakes: 3/4 teaspoon
• Salt: 1 teaspoon
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Large baking dish (or 2 smaller ones), 2 bowls, thermometer

GET READY
1. Put the oven shelf in the middle. Turn the oven on to 190°C.
2. Squeeze 3 limes into a large bowl. You need about 90 ml of juice. Remove the seeds.
3. Cut the 4th lime into thin rounds.
4. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
5. Add the olive oil, the garlic, the oregano and the chili flakes to the lime juice.
6. Whisk with a fork for 20 seconds.
7. Cut the feta into cubes of about 1 cm.
8. Add the feta to the bowl and toss gently so every cube is coated.
9. Cover the bowl and put it in the fridge.
10. Peel the red onions. Cut one into thin half-rings. Chop the other into small cubes of about 5 mm. Keep them in two separate piles.
11. Cut the tomatoes into cubes of about 2 cm.
12. Cut the cucumber into cubes of about 1 cm.
13. Cut each olive in half.
14. Chop the dill finely.

BAKE
15. Put the lime rounds and the onion half-rings on the bottom of the baking dish.
16. Pat the salmon dry with paper towel.
17. Put the salmon on top of the onions, skin side down, with space between the pieces.
18. Sprinkle 1 teaspoon of salt and 1/2 teaspoon of pepper over the 6 pieces.
19. Put the dish in the middle of the hot oven.
20. Bake for 18 to 20 minutes.
21. Push the thermometer into the thickest piece. Take the dish out at 60°C. If it reads less, bake 2 more minutes and check again.

FETA TOPPING (while the salmon bakes)
22. Take the bowl with the feta out of the fridge.
23. Add the tomatoes, cucumber, olives, chopped onion and dill.
24. Fold gently with a spoon, 4 or 5 turns. Do not crush the feta.

SERVE
25. Move the salmon with a wide spatula onto a large platter. Add the lime rounds and onions.
26. Spoon the feta topping over the salmon.
✅ READY WHEN: the salmon flakes easily with a fork and is pale pink all the way through, and the topping is fresh and chunky.
⚠️ Do not bake past 60°C. Salmon turns dry and chalky.
⚠️ Feta and olives are salty. Do not add any more salt to the topping.

🍔 CHEESEBURGER CABBAGE WRAPS — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A burger wrapped in a cabbage leaf instead of a bun.
⏱ 45 minutes (pot, pan 1 and pan 2). Start at T minus 50 min.

YOU NEED
• Lean ground beef: 900 g (6 patties of 150 g)
• Cabbage: 1 head (you need 6 large whole leaves)
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 120 g (6 slices of 20 g)
• Red onion: 100 g (1 small)
• Tomatoes: 200 g (2 medium)
• Pickled cucumbers: 100 g
• Mayonnaise: 60 g (4 tablespoons)
• Mustard: 3 tablespoons
• Vinegar: 1 1/2 teaspoons
• Garlic: 5 g (1 small clove)
• Salt: 1 3/4 teaspoons (1 1/2 for the patties, 1/4 for the sauce)
• Black pepper: 3/4 teaspoon plus 1 pinch
• Vegetable oil: 10 ml (2 teaspoons)
• Large pot, 2 large pans with a lid or foil, tongs, spatula, thermometer, paper towels

GET READY
1. Peel the garlic, crush it with the flat side of a knife and chop it finely.
2. Mix the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, garlic, 1/4 teaspoon of salt and 1 pinch of pepper in a bowl. Whisk until smooth.
3. Cover the sauce and put it in the fridge.
4. Cut the red onion into thin rounds.
5. Cut the tomatoes into slices about 5 mm thick.
6. Cut the pickled cucumbers into slices about 3 mm thick.
7. Cut the cheese into 6 slices.
8. Divide the beef into 6 equal balls (150 g each).
9. Press each ball into a flat patty about 2 cm thick and 10 cm across.
10. Press a shallow dimple into the middle of each patty with your thumb. This stops the patty from puffing up.
11. Sprinkle the patties with 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt and 3/4 teaspoon of pepper in total, on both sides.

PREPARE THE CABBAGE LEAVES
12. Cut the hard white core out of the bottom of the cabbage head.
13. Peel off 6 large whole leaves without tearing them.
14. Bring a large pot of water to a boil.
15. Using tongs, dip one leaf into the boiling water for 30 seconds. Do the same with the other 5 leaves.
16. Put the leaves on a plate lined with paper towel and pat them dry.
17. With a knife, shave down the thick white rib at the bottom of each leaf so that it is flatter. This helps the leaf fold.

COOK THE BURGERS (2 pans, 3 patties in each)
18. Heat both pans on medium-high for 3 minutes.
19. Add 1 teaspoon (5 ml) of oil to each pan.
20. Lay 3 patties in each pan, not touching each other.
21. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes without moving them, until deep brown on the bottom.
22. Turn each patty once with a spatula.
23. Cook for 4 to 5 more minutes.
24. Push the thermometer sideways into the middle of a patty. It must read at least 74°C. If it reads less, cook 1 more minute and check again.
25. In the last minute, put one slice of cheese on each patty.
26. Cover the pans with a lid or foil until the cheese melts.

ASSEMBLE
27. Lay one cabbage leaf flat, with the thick end toward you.
28. Put a burger on the thick end of the leaf.
29. Put 1 onion round, 1 slice of tomato and 2 or 3 pickle slices on the burger.
30. Fold the thick end of the leaf up over the burger.
31. Fold one side of the leaf over the burger.
32. Roll the burger toward the other end of the leaf, until it is fully wrapped. Leave it seam side down.
33. Repeat with the other 5 burgers.
34. Put the wraps on a platter. Put the sauce in a bowl with a spoon next to them.
✅ READY WHEN: each patty is brown and juicy and reads 74°C, the cheese is melted, and the leaf is soft, bright green and not torn.
⚠️ Do not skip the boiling water. Raw cabbage is stiff and breaks when you fold it.
⚠️ Ground beef must reach 74°C in the middle. Use the thermometer.

🍕 PIZZA FRITTATA — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Baked in the oven in a dish, cut into squares.
⏱ 50 minutes (pan 1 and the oven). Start at T minus 55 min.

YOU NEED
• Eggs: 12
• Napoletana tomato sauce: 450 g
• Baby spinach: 100 g
• Onion: 100 g (1 medium)
• Garlic: 5 g (1 clove)
• Mozzarella: 120 g
• Salami: 60 g
• Milk: 45 ml (3 tablespoons)
• Dried oregano: 1 1/2 teaspoons
• Olive oil: 35 ml (2 tablespoons for the pan and 1 teaspoon for the dish)
• Salt: 1 1/4 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Baking dish about 23 x 33 cm, large pan, big bowl, whisk or fork, thermometer

GET READY
1. Put the oven shelf in the middle. Turn the oven on to 200°C.
2. Rub the baking dish with 1 teaspoon of olive oil.
3. Peel the onion and cut it into small cubes of about 5 mm.
4. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
5. Grate the mozzarella on the large holes of the grater.
6. Cut the salami into thin rounds, about 3 mm.
7. Crack the 12 eggs into a big bowl.
8. Add the milk, the oregano, 1 teaspoon of salt, the pepper and the tomato sauce.
9. Whisk with a fork for 30 seconds, until the colour is even.

COOK THE VEGETABLES
10. Heat 2 tablespoons (30 ml) of olive oil in the large pan on medium heat.
11. Add the onion and the garlic. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring, until soft.
12. Add the spinach. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring, until it is wilted.
13. Sprinkle 1/4 teaspoon of salt over the spinach and stir.
14. Spread the vegetables evenly on the bottom of the baking dish.

BAKE
15. Pour the egg mixture over the vegetables.
16. Scatter the mozzarella and the salami on top.
17. Put the dish in the middle of the hot oven.
18. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes. Do not open the oven in the first 20 minutes.
19. Shake the dish gently. The middle must not wobble.
20. Push the thermometer into the middle. It must read at least 72°C. If it wobbles or reads less, bake 5 more minutes and check again.
21. Take the dish out and let it rest for 5 minutes.
22. Cut it into 6 big squares (3 columns, 2 rows) and put them on a platter.
✅ READY WHEN: puffed up and golden at the edges, set in the middle, the cheese is melted, and the thermometer reads 72°C or more.
⚠️ Opening the oven early makes the frittata fall.
⚠️ If the salami starts to burn, cover the dish with foil.

🥓 LOADED CAULIFLOWER BREAKFAST BAKE — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Cauliflower, bacon, cheese and eggs baked in a dish, cut into squares.
⏱ 65 minutes (pan 1 and the oven). Start at T minus 70 min.

YOU NEED
• Cauliflower: 1000 g (1 large head)
• Eggs: 12
• Bacon: 240 g (about 8 slices)
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 200 g
• Milk: 240 ml (1 cup)
• Red onion: 50 g
• Garlic: 10 g (2 cloves)
• Paprika: 2 teaspoons
• Salt: 1 1/2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Olive oil: 1 teaspoon, for the dish
• Baking dish about 23 x 33 cm, box grater, large pan, big bowl, thermometer, paper towels

GET READY
1. Put the oven shelf in the middle. Turn the oven on to 180°C.
2. Rub the baking dish with the olive oil.
3. Cut the cauliflower head into 4 pieces.
4. Grate the cauliflower on the large holes of the grater, straight into the baking dish.
5. If the grated cauliflower looks wet, press it between sheets of paper towel.
6. Spread the cauliflower evenly in the dish and press it down lightly.
7. Cut the bacon into strips about 1 cm wide.
8. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
9. Peel the red onion and chop it into small cubes of about 5 mm.
10. Grate the cheese on the large holes of the grater.

COOK THE BACON
11. Put the bacon in the cold pan. Turn the heat to medium.
12. Fry for 8 minutes, stirring every minute, until crispy.
13. Move the bacon to a plate lined with paper towel.

BAKE
14. Crack the eggs into a big bowl.
15. Add the milk, the garlic, the paprika, the salt and the pepper.
16. Whisk with a fork for 30 seconds, until the colour is even.
17. Sprinkle the cheese over the cauliflower in the dish.
18. Scatter the bacon over the cheese.
19. Scatter the red onion over the bacon.
20. Pour the egg mixture evenly over everything.
21. Put the dish in the middle of the hot oven.
22. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes.
23. Shake the dish gently. The middle must not wobble, and the top must be golden.
24. Push the thermometer into the middle. It must read at least 72°C. If it reads less, bake 5 more minutes and check again.
25. Take the dish out and let it rest for 5 minutes.
26. Cut it into 6 big squares and put them on a platter.
✅ READY WHEN: golden on top, set in the middle, and the thermometer reads 72°C or more.
⚠️ Do not skip step 5. Wet cauliflower makes the bake watery.
⚠️ Bacon burns fast. Stir it often and take it off as soon as it is crispy.

🌭 SAUSAGE AND BROCCOLI EGG CASSEROLE — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Sausage, broccoli and cheese baked in cream and eggs, cut into squares.
⏱ 80 minutes (pan 1, pot and the oven). Start at T minus 85 min.

YOU NEED
• Sausages (the pre-cooked kind): 900 g
• Broccoli: 300 g
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 115 g
• Eggs: 12
• Cream: 360 ml (1 1/2 cups)
• Garlic: 5 g (1 clove)
• Olive oil: 35 ml (2 tablespoons for the pan and 1 teaspoon for the dish)
• Salt: 1 tablespoon (for the water) + 1 1/4 teaspoons (for the eggs)
• Black pepper: 1/4 teaspoon
• Baking dish about 23 x 33 cm, large pan, pot, colander, clean kitchen towel, big bowl, thermometer

GET READY
1. Put the oven shelf in the middle. Turn the oven on to 180°C.
2. Rub the baking dish with 1 teaspoon of olive oil.
3. Cut the sausages into coins about 1 cm thick.
4. Cut the broccoli into small florets of 2 to 3 cm.
5. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
6. Grate the cheese on the large holes of the grater.

COOK THE SAUSAGES
7. Heat 2 tablespoons (30 ml) of olive oil in the large pan on medium heat.
8. Add the sausage coins.
9. Fry for 8 minutes, stirring every minute, until golden.
10. Add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
11. Move everything to a large bowl.

COOK THE BROCCOLI
12. Pour 3 litres of water into the pot and bring it to a boil.
13. Add 1 tablespoon (18 g) of salt.
14. Add the broccoli and boil for 3 minutes.
15. Pour the broccoli into the colander and rinse with cold water for 30 seconds.
16. Wrap the broccoli in the clean kitchen towel and squeeze out as much water as you can.
17. Chop the broccoli into pieces of about 1 cm.
18. Add the broccoli to the bowl with the sausages and mix.

BAKE
19. Spread the sausage and broccoli mix evenly in the baking dish.
20. Sprinkle the cheese over the top.
21. Crack the eggs into a big bowl.
22. Add the cream, 1 1/4 teaspoons of salt and the pepper.
23. Whisk with a fork for 30 seconds, until the colour is even.
24. Pour the egg mixture over the dish.
25. Put the dish in the middle of the hot oven.
26. Bake for 45 to 55 minutes.
27. Shake the dish gently. The middle must not wobble.
28. Push the thermometer into the middle. It must read at least 72°C. If it reads less, bake 5 more minutes and check again.
29. Take the dish out and let it rest for 15 minutes. It firms up while it rests.
30. Cut it into 6 big squares and put them on a platter.
✅ READY WHEN: golden on top, set in the middle, and the thermometer reads 72°C or more.
⚠️ Do not skip the squeezing in step 16. Wet broccoli makes the casserole watery.
⚠️ Cut it only after the 15 minute rest, or it falls apart.
