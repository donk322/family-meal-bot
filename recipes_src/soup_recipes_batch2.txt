🧆 MEATBALL SOUP WITH VEGETABLES — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 60 minutes (large pot, 1 pan). Start at T minus 65 min.

YOU NEED
• Lean ground beef: 700 g
• Cauliflower: 400 g
• Carrots: 100 g
• Onion: 150 g
• Garlic: 10 g (2 cloves)
• Egg: 1
• Tomato paste: 20 g (1 tablespoon)
• Butter: 10 g
• Bay leaf: 1
• Dill: 5 g
• Water: 2800 ml
• Salt: 2 1/2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 3/4 teaspoon
• Large pot, 1 pan, bowl, grater, slotted spoon, thermometer

GET READY
1. Peel the onion. Chop half of it very finely. Chop the other half into small cubes. Keep them in two piles.
2. Peel the garlic and grate it on the small holes.
3. Peel the carrots and grate them on the large holes.
4. Cut the cauliflower into small florets of about 3 cm.
5. Chop the dill finely.

MEATBALLS
6. Put the beef, the finely chopped half of the onion, the garlic, the egg, 1 teaspoon of salt and 1/2 teaspoon of pepper in a bowl.
7. Mix with your hands for 1 minute, until just combined.
8. Wet your hands with water. Make 32 small balls, about 20 g each.

SOUP
9. Pour 2800 ml of water into the large pot. Add 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt and the bay leaf.
10. Bring to a boil.
11. Lower the meatballs into the boiling water one at a time with a spoon.
12. When it boils again, turn the heat to low. Skim off the foam.
13. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes.
14. Push the thermometer into a meatball. It must read at least 74°C.
15. Melt the butter in the pan on medium heat. Add the cubed onion and the carrots. Fry for 5 minutes.
16. Add the tomato paste. Stir for 1 minute.
17. Add the fried vegetables and the cauliflower to the pot.
18. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes, until the cauliflower is soft.
19. Turn off the heat. Add the dill. Cover the pot and leave it for 10 minutes.
✅ READY WHEN: the meatballs are firm and reach 74°C, the cauliflower is soft, and the broth is clear and orange.
⚠️ Lower the meatballs gently. If you drop them, they break.
⚠️ Do not stir hard in the first 5 minutes. The meatballs are still soft.

🍄 CLEAR MUSHROOM SOUP (Friday, no meat) — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 45 minutes (large pot). Start at T minus 50 min.

YOU NEED
• Mushrooms (any mix): 600 g
• Onion: 150 g
• Carrots: 100 g
• Garlic: 10 g (2 cloves)
• Butter: 30 g
• Dill: 5 g
• Bay leaf: 1
• Black peppercorns: 5
• Water: 2800 ml
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Large pot, grater, slotted spoon

GET READY
1. Wipe the mushrooms with a damp paper towel. Do not soak them in water.
2. Cut the mushrooms into slices about 5 mm thick.
3. Peel the onion and cut it into small cubes. Peel the carrots and grate them on the large holes.
4. Peel the garlic and chop it finely. Chop the dill finely.

COOK
5. Melt the butter in the large pot on medium heat.
6. Add the onion and the carrots. Fry for 3 to 5 minutes until soft and golden.
7. Add the mushrooms.
8. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring, until all the water has gone and the mushrooms are lightly browned.
9. Pour in 2800 ml of hot water.
10. Add the bay leaf, the peppercorns and 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt.
11. Bring to a boil. Turn the heat to low.
12. Cook for 15 to 20 minutes.
13. Add the garlic, the dill and the black pepper.
14. Cover the pot and leave it for 10 minutes.
15. Take out the bay leaf. Taste the soup. If it needs salt, add 1/2 teaspoon, stir for 1 minute and taste again.
✅ READY WHEN: the mushrooms are soft, the broth is clear and amber-brown, and it smells of mushrooms.
⚠️ Do not cook the mushrooms longer than 20 minutes in the water. They turn tough.
⚠️ Browning the mushrooms in step 8 gives the soup its flavour. Do not skip it.

🍲 CLASSIC BORSCHT WITH BEEF — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ About 2 hours (large pot, 1 large pan). Start at T minus 130 min.

YOU NEED
• Beef (stewing pieces): 640 g
• Beetroot: 800 g
• Carrots: 320 g
• Onions: 240 g
• Tomato paste: 160 g
• Vegetable stock cubes: 3
• Water: 2400 ml
• Vegetable oil: 45 ml (3 tablespoons)
• Salt: about 1 teaspoon (see the last steps)
• Large pot (4 litres or bigger), large frying pan, grater, slotted spoon

PART 1 - THE BEEF (about 1 hour 30 min)
1. Cut the beef into cubes of about 2 x 2 cm.
2. Pour 2400 ml of COLD water into the large pot. Add the beef.
3. Put the pot on high heat and bring to a boil.
4. Skim the grey foam off the top with a slotted spoon. Keep skimming for about 5 minutes.
5. Turn the heat to low so it only bubbles gently. Put the lid on.
6. Cook for 40 to 50 minutes, then test a piece of beef with a fork.
✅ BEEF IS READY WHEN: a fork goes in easily and the meat nearly falls apart.
7. If it is still hard, cook and test again every 10 minutes. Stewing beef can need up to 1.5 hours.

PART 2 - THE VEGETABLES (while the beef cooks)
8. Peel the beetroot, the carrots and the onions.
9. Grate the beetroot and the carrots on the large holes of the grater. Your hands will turn red.
10. Cut the onions into small cubes of about 5 mm.
11. Heat the oil in the large frying pan on medium heat for 1 minute.
12. Add the onions. Fry for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring.
13. Add the carrots and the beetroot. Fry for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring every minute.
14. Add the tomato paste and stir. Fry for 2 more minutes.

PART 3 - PUT IT TOGETHER
15. When the beef is soft, scrape all the fried vegetables into the pot.
16. Add the 3 stock cubes. Stir until they dissolve.
17. Simmer on low heat for 15 minutes.
18. Taste a piece of beetroot. It must be soft.
19. Taste the soup. If it needs salt, add 1/2 teaspoon, stir for 1 minute and taste again. Usually 1 teaspoon is enough, because the stock cubes are salty.
20. Turn the heat to the lowest setting and keep the pot covered until serving.
✅ READY WHEN: the beef is soft, the beetroot is soft, and the soup is deep red.
⚠️ Do not add salt before the stock cubes dissolve. Too much salt cannot be fixed.

🍜 BEEF PHO WITH SHIRATAKI NOODLES — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A Vietnamese-style soup. The broth simmers for an hour with spices. The beef is cut paper-thin and cooked in the hot broth at the table.
⏱ About 90 minutes (large pot). Start at T minus 95 min.

YOU NEED
• Flank steak (or any lean beef steak): 700 g
• Shirataki noodles: 700 g
• Onion: 300 g
• Garlic: 25 g (5 cloves)
• Fresh ginger: 5 cm piece
• Cinnamon sticks: 6
• Star anise: 4
• Cloves: 12
• Cardamom pods: 5
• Fish sauce: 45 ml (3 tablespoons, gluten-free)
• Vegetable oil: 15 ml (1 tablespoon)
• Water: 4000 ml
• Beef stock cube: 1 (or mushroom stock cube)
• Bean sprouts: 150 g
• Basil: 1 large bunch
• Jalapeño chili: 2
• Limes: 2
• Large pot, slotted spoon, sharp knife, colander, big platter

GET READY
1. Put the steak in the freezer for 30 minutes. It must be firm outside and still a little soft inside.
2. Peel the onion and cut it into 8 wedges. Peel the garlic and smash each clove with the flat side of a knife.
3. Slice the ginger into thin rounds.
4. Cut the limes into wedges. Slice the jalapeños into thin rings.
5. Pick the basil leaves off the stems.

BROTH
6. Heat the oil in the large pot on medium heat.
7. Add the onion, the ginger, the garlic, the cinnamon, the star anise, the cloves and the cardamom.
8. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring, until the onion browns on all sides.
9. Pour in 4000 ml of water. Add the stock cube and the fish sauce.
10. Bring to a boil. Turn the heat to low so it only bubbles gently.
11. Cook for 60 minutes.

BEEF AND NOODLES
12. Take the steak out of the freezer. Cut it ACROSS the lines of the meat into very thin slices, about 2 mm.
13. Lay the slices on a platter in one layer. Put the sprouts, basil, jalapeños and limes on another platter.
14. Rinse the noodles in a colander under cold water for 2 minutes.
15. With a slotted spoon, lift all the onion and spices out of the broth.
16. Add the noodles to the broth. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes.
17. Turn the heat to very low.
18. Put the steak slices into the hot broth one by one.
19. Stir gently for 30 seconds. The meat turns grey-brown. Turn the heat off.
✅ READY WHEN: the broth is clear and brown and smells of cinnamon and anise, and the beef slices are cooked but still tender.
⚠️ Slice the beef very thin and cook it only 30 seconds. Thick or long-cooked slices turn tough.
⚠️ Fish sauce smells strong. This is normal. Do not skip it.
