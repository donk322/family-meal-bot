🥚 PULLED PORK EGGS BENEDICT — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A long dish. The pork cooks slowly in the oven, the eggs are poached at the end.
⏱ About 4 hours (oven, 1 large pot, 1 large pan, 1 bowl). Start at T minus 250 min.

YOU NEED
• Pork shoulder (boneless): 1350 g
• Gluten-free English muffins: 6
• Eggs: 12 (for poaching) + 4 egg yolks (for the sauce)
• Butter: 225 g
• Lime juice: 2 tablespoons
• Vinegar: 2 tablespoons (for the poaching water)
• Paprika: 1 teaspoon
• Salt: 3 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• Olive oil: 1 tablespoon
• Water: 1 tablespoon (warm, for the sauce)
• Baking dish with foil, thermometer, large pot, large pan, heatproof bowl, whisk, slotted spoon, 2 forks

PORK (starts at T minus 250 min)
1. Turn the oven on to 150°C.
2. Pat the pork dry with paper towel. Cut it into 4 big pieces.
3. Mix 2 teaspoons of salt, the pepper and the paprika in a small bowl.
4. Rub the olive oil and then the spice mix over all the pork pieces.
5. Put the pork in a baking dish. Cover the dish tightly with foil.
6. Bake for 3 hours.
7. Push the thermometer into the thickest piece. It must read at least 90°C and the meat must fall apart when pressed with a fork.
8. If it is harder, cover again and bake 30 more minutes, then check again.
9. Take the dish out. Let it rest covered for 15 minutes.
10. Pull the meat apart into shreds with two forks. Mix it with the juice from the dish.
11. Cover with foil and keep it warm in the switched-off oven.

SAUCE (at T minus 30 min)
12. Melt the butter in a small pot on low heat. Keep it warm.
13. Half-fill a pot with water and bring it to a gentle simmer. Do not boil hard.
14. Put the 4 egg yolks, the lime juice and 1 tablespoon of warm water in a heatproof bowl.
15. Put the bowl on top of the pot, not touching the water.
16. Whisk all the time for 2 to 3 minutes, until the yolks are pale and thick.
17. Take the bowl off the heat.
18. Whisk in the warm butter a little at a time, a teaspoon at first, then faster.
19. Whisk in 1 teaspoon of salt.
20. Cover the bowl and put it on a warm spot, not on the stove.

POACHED EGGS (at T minus 20 min, 3 batches of 4 eggs)
21. Fill the large pan 8 cm deep with water. Add the vinegar. Bring to a gentle simmer.
22. Crack each egg into a small cup.
23. Stir the water in a circle to make a slow whirlpool.
24. Slide 4 eggs into the water, one at a time, gently.
25. Cook for 3 minutes for a runny yolk.
26. Lift the eggs out with a slotted spoon onto a plate lined with paper towel.
27. Repeat steps 23 to 26 until all 12 eggs are done.

BUILD
28. Split the muffins and toast all halves.
29. Put 2 halves on each plate.
30. Put a heap of pulled pork on each half.
31. Put a poached egg on the pork.
32. Spoon the sauce over each egg.
✅ READY WHEN: the pork is tender and shreds easily, the egg white is set and the yolk runs when cut, and the sauce is smooth and yellow.
⚠️ The sauce splits if it gets too hot. If it looks curdled, whisk in 1 teaspoon of warm water.
⚠️ Eggs go cold fast. Poach them last and serve at once.
⚠️ Do not add salt to the poaching water.

🌯 BACON, EGG AND CHEESE BREAKFAST BURRITOS — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 55 minutes (oven, pan 1, pan 2). Start at T minus 60 min.

YOU NEED
• Eggs: 12
• Bacon: 450 g (about 15 slices)
• Gluten-free flatbreads (large, soft): 6
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 270 g
• Cream: 180 ml
• Butter: 30 g
• Salt: 3/4 teaspoon
• Black pepper: 3/4 teaspoon
• Baking dish about 23 x 33 cm, 2 pans, whisk, foil, paper towels

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 180°C.
2. Rub the baking dish with a little butter.
3. Grate the cheese on the large holes of the grater.
4. Crack the eggs into a bowl. Add the cream, the salt and the pepper. Whisk for 20 seconds.

BACON (pan 1, in batches)
5. Lay 5 bacon slices in pan 1, one layer, cold pan. Turn the heat to medium.
6. Fry for 4 to 5 minutes, turn, fry 3 to 4 more minutes until crispy.
7. Move the bacon to paper towel. Repeat until all the bacon is done.
8. Crumble the cooled bacon into small pieces.

EGGS (pan 2)
9. Melt the butter in pan 2 on LOW heat.
10. Pour in all the eggs. Wait 20 seconds.
11. Stir slowly for 5 to 6 minutes until soft and creamy.
12. Take the pan off the heat while the eggs are still a little wet. They finish cooking in the heat.

BUILD
13. Warm each flatbread for 10 seconds in a dry pan so it bends without breaking.
14. Put the flatbread flat. Spoon the eggs down the middle, about 1/6 of the eggs.
15. Put bacon and 30 g of cheese on the eggs.
16. Fold the sides in, then roll the flatbread tight.
17. Put the burrito seam side down in the baking dish. Repeat for all 6.
18. Sprinkle the rest of the cheese and some bacon on top.
19. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbling.
✅ READY WHEN: the cheese on top is melted and golden, and the burritos are hot all the way through.
⚠️ Do not overcook the eggs in step 11. They bake again in the oven.
⚠️ Make sure the flatbreads are gluten-free.

🍳 CHEESE FRICO FRIED EGG TOSTADAS — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A light dish. Crispy cheese discs with a fried egg and fresh tomato salsa.
⏱ 35 minutes (pan 1 and pan 2). Start at T minus 40 min.

YOU NEED
• Eggs: 6
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 180 g
• Tomatoes: 250 g (about 2 medium)
• Red onion: 50 g
• Lime juice: 2 tablespoons
• Chili flakes: 1/4 teaspoon
• Salt: 3/4 teaspoon
• Black pepper: 1/4 teaspoon
• 2 small non-stick pans with lids (or foil), baking tray, baking paper, spatula

GET READY
1. Cut the tomatoes into small cubes of about 5 mm. Cut the red onion into small cubes.
2. Mix the tomatoes, onion, lime juice, chili flakes and 1/2 teaspoon of salt in a bowl.
3. Cover the bowl and put it in the fridge.
4. Grate the cheese on the large holes of the grater.
5. Cover a baking tray with baking paper.

COOK (6 discs, 3 in each pan, one disc at a time)
6. Heat the small non-stick pan on medium heat for 1 minute. Do not add oil.
7. Sprinkle 30 g of cheese in an even round layer, about 12 cm wide.
8. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes without touching it, until the cheese is melted and bubbling.
9. Crack 1 egg into the middle of the cheese.
10. Sprinkle a tiny pinch of salt and pepper over the egg.
11. Cook for 2 minutes without touching it, until the egg white is half set and the cheese is deep golden.
12. Cover the pan and cook for 1 more minute, until the egg white is fully set.
13. Take the lid off. Cook 1 more minute, until the cheese is firm enough to lift.
14. Lift the disc with a spatula onto the baking tray. Let it harden for 2 minutes.
15. Repeat steps 7 to 14 for the other 5 discs. Use the second pan at the same time.
16. Put the discs on a platter. Spoon the cold salsa on each one.
✅ READY WHEN: the cheese is crisp and deep golden, the egg white is set and the yolk is soft, and the disc holds its shape when lifted.
⚠️ Do not use oil. The cheese makes its own fat.
⚠️ If the cheese sticks, wait 1 more minute. It lifts off when it is crisp.

🥞 CREAM CHEESE PANCAKES (no flour) — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Thin pancakes made of almond flour, cream cheese and eggs. 3 pancakes per portion.
⏱ 40 minutes (pan 1 and pan 2). Start at T minus 45 min.

YOU NEED
• Eggs: 9
• Cream cheese: 260 g
• Almond flour: 110 g
• Butter: 60 g
• Lime zest: 2 teaspoons (from 1 lime)
• Vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon
• 2 non-stick pans, whisk, big bowl, spatula

GET READY
1. Let the cream cheese stand for 20 minutes at room temperature so it is soft.
2. Grate the zest of 1 lime on the small holes of the grater. You need 2 teaspoons.
3. Crack the eggs into a big bowl.
4. Add the cream cheese, the almond flour, the lime zest and the vanilla.
5. Whisk for 1 to 2 minutes until the batter is smooth, with no lumps.

COOK (2 pans, 3 pancakes at a time in each pan)
6. Heat both pans on medium-low heat for 1 minute.
7. Melt 1 teaspoon of butter in each pan.
8. Pour 3 tablespoons of batter into the pan for each pancake. Put 3 pancakes in each pan.
9. Cook for 2 minutes, until golden underneath and the edges look set.
10. Flip each pancake with a spatula.
11. Cook for 2 more minutes.
12. Move the pancakes to a warm platter and cover with foil.
13. Repeat steps 7 to 12 until all the batter is used (about 18 pancakes).
14. Serve with a small piece of butter on each stack.
✅ READY WHEN: golden on both sides, set in the middle, and they flip without breaking.
⚠️ Keep the heat medium-low. High heat burns the outside and leaves the middle raw.
⚠️ The batter is thin and the pancakes are soft. They are not like normal pancakes. Flip them gently.

🐟 SMOKED SALMON AND CREAM CHEESE ROLLS — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A light cold dish. Make it a few hours ahead.
⏱ 30 minutes of work, then 2 hours in the fridge. Start at T minus 150 min.

YOU NEED
• Smoked salmon, sliced: 270 g
• Cream cheese: 375 g
• Pickled cucumbers: 3 small
• Red onion: 75 g
• Lime zest: 1 lime
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Baking paper (greaseproof), cling film, sharp knife, rolling pin or a bottle, bowl

GET READY
1. Let the cream cheese stand for 20 minutes at room temperature so it is soft.
2. Cut the pickled cucumbers into very small cubes of about 3 mm.
3. Cut the red onion into very small cubes of about 3 mm.
4. Grate the zest of 1 lime on the small holes of the grater.
5. Mix the cream cheese, the cucumbers, the onion, the lime zest and the pepper in a bowl until smooth. Do not add salt.

ROLL (2 rolls)
6. Cut 2 sheets of baking paper, each about 40 cm long.
7. Put the salmon slices on one sheet, slightly overlapping, in a rectangle about 30 x 15 cm. Use half of the salmon for each roll.
8. Put the second sheet on top and press or roll with a rolling pin to flatten the salmon.
9. Remove the top sheet.
10. Spoon half of the cream cheese mix in a line down the middle of the salmon, about 4 cm wide.
11. Using the bottom paper, lift one long side of the salmon over the filling.
12. Keep rolling it into a tight log. Peel the paper away as you roll.
13. Wrap the roll in cling film and twist the ends.
14. Repeat for the second roll.
15. Put both rolls in the fridge for 2 hours.

SERVE
16. Unwrap the rolls.
17. Cut each roll into rounds about 2 cm thick with a sharp knife, wiping the knife between cuts.
18. Put the rounds on a platter.
✅ READY WHEN: the rolls are firm, the cut rounds hold their shape, and the filling is pale with small green and purple pieces.
⚠️ Do not add salt. Salmon and cream cheese are already salty.
⚠️ Use a very sharp knife. A dull knife squashes the rolls.
