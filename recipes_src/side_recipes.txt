🧀 CAULIFLOWER MAC AND CHEESE — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Roasted cauliflower in a very cheesy sauce with a crunchy top. No pasta.
⏱ 85 minutes (oven, 2 baking trays, large pot). Start at T minus 90 min.

YOU NEED
• Cauliflower: 1400 g (2 medium heads)
• Cream: 240 ml
• Cream cheese: 170 g
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 340 g
• Mozzarella: 220 g
• Parmesan: 30 g
• Pork rinds (crushed): 115 g
• Olive oil: 45 ml (3 tablespoons)
• Arrowroot: 1 tablespoon
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• 2 baking trays, baking dish about 23 x 33 cm, large pot, big bowl

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 190°C. Put the shelves in the top and the middle.
2. Cut the cauliflower into florets of about 4 cm.
3. Cut the cream cheese into cubes of 2 cm.
4. Grate the cheddar and the mozzarella on the large holes. Grate the parmesan on the small holes.
5. Crush the pork rinds into small pieces in a bag with a rolling pin or a bottle.

ROAST THE CAULIFLOWER
6. Put the florets in a big bowl with 2 tablespoons (30 ml) of oil and 1 teaspoon of salt. Toss.
7. Spread them over the 2 baking trays in one layer.
8. Roast for 35 to 40 minutes, swapping the trays after 20 minutes, until tender and golden.
9. Let the cauliflower cool for 10 minutes.

CHEESE SAUCE (while the cauliflower roasts)
10. Pour the cream into the large pot. Heat on medium until small bubbles appear. Do not boil.
11. Turn the heat to low. Add the cream cheese, the cheddar and the mozzarella.
12. Stir until all the cheese is melted and the sauce is smooth.
13. Mix the arrowroot with 3 tablespoons of cold water in a cup until smooth.
14. Take the pot off the heat. Stir in the arrowroot mix.
15. Add 1 teaspoon of salt and the pepper.

BAKE
16. Fold the cauliflower into the sauce.
17. Pour everything into the baking dish.
18. Mix the pork rinds, the parmesan and 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of oil in a bowl. Sprinkle it over the dish.
19. Bake for 15 minutes, until the top is golden.
20. Let it rest for 5 minutes.
✅ READY WHEN: the top is golden and crunchy, the sauce bubbles at the edges, and the cauliflower is soft.
⚠️ Do not boil the cheese sauce. It splits and turns oily.
⚠️ Roast the cauliflower on two trays, not one. A crowded tray steams it.

🧆 CAULIFLOWER TOTS WITH SPICY DIP — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
About 40 small tots, 5 per portion.
⏱ 60 minutes (steamer or pot, oven). Start at T minus 65 min.

YOU NEED
• Cauliflower: 900 g
• Eggs: 2
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 120 g
• Parmesan: 100 g
• Pork rinds (crushed finely): 80 g
• Salt: 1 teaspoon
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• For the dip: mayonnaise 100 g, paprika 1 teaspoon, chili flakes 1/4 teaspoon
• Pot with a colander or steamer, clean kitchen towel, baking tray, baking paper, big bowl

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 200°C. Cover the baking tray with baking paper.
2. Cut the cauliflower into small florets.
3. Grate the cheddar and the parmesan on the small holes.
4. Crush the pork rinds into fine crumbs in a bag with a rolling pin.
5. Mix the dip in a small bowl: mayonnaise, paprika and chili flakes. Cover it and put it in the fridge.

STEAM AND SQUEEZE
6. Put the florets in a colander over a pot of boiling water, with a lid on top. Steam for 8 minutes until soft.
7. Let the cauliflower cool for 10 minutes.
8. Chop it very finely with a sharp knife into pieces the size of rice grains.
9. Put the chopped cauliflower in a clean kitchen towel. Twist and squeeze out as much water as you can.

MIX AND SHAPE
10. Put the cauliflower, the eggs, the cheddar, the parmesan, the pork rinds, the salt and the pepper in a big bowl.
11. Mix with your hands until everything holds together.
12. Take 1 tablespoon of the mix and roll it into a small barrel, like a tater tot.
13. Put the tots on the tray, with a little space between them. Make about 40.

BAKE
14. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes, until golden.
15. Put the tots on a platter. Put the dip next to them.
✅ READY WHEN: the tots are golden and crisp on the outside and hold their shape when you lift them.
⚠️ Squeeze the water out well. Wet cauliflower makes tots that fall apart.
⚠️ If the mix is too wet to roll, add 1 more tablespoon of parmesan.

🥬 CREAMED BRUSSELS SPROUTS WITH CHEESE — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 70 minutes (pot, large pan, oven). Start at T minus 75 min.

YOU NEED
• Brussels sprouts: 1000 g
• Cream: 240 ml
• Sour cream: 120 ml
• Parmesan: 50 g
• Mozzarella: 120 g
• Red onion: 100 g
• Butter: 30 g
• Garlic: 10 g (2 cloves)
• Salt: 1 tablespoon for the water + 1 1/2 teaspoons for the sauce
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• Large pot, large pan, big bowl with ice, colander, baking dish about 23 x 33 cm

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 190°C.
2. Cut the hard end off each sprout. Cut each sprout in half from top to bottom.
3. Peel the red onion and cut it into very small cubes. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
4. Grate the parmesan on the small holes and the mozzarella on the large holes.
5. Fill a big bowl with ice and cold water.

BLANCH
6. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add 1 tablespoon of salt.
7. Put the sprouts in the water. Boil for 2 minutes, until they turn bright green.
8. Drain them in the colander and put them in the ice water for 5 minutes.
9. Drain them well and put them in the baking dish.

SAUCE
10. Melt the butter in the large pan on medium-low heat.
11. Add the onion and the garlic. Cook for 4 minutes, stirring, until soft.
12. Add the pepper. Pour in the cream and the sour cream.
13. Add 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt. Stir. Take the pan off the heat.
14. Add the parmesan and half of the mozzarella (60 g). Stir until melted and smooth.

BAKE
15. Pour the sauce over the sprouts and toss to coat.
16. Sprinkle the rest of the mozzarella (60 g) on top.
17. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until bubbling and golden.
✅ READY WHEN: the top is golden, the sauce bubbles at the edges, and the sprouts are tender when you push a fork in.
⚠️ Do not boil the sprouts longer than 2 minutes. They turn grey and smell strong.
⚠️ The ice water in step 8 keeps them green. Do not skip it.

🥒 GARLIC PARMESAN ZUCCHINI — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 25 minutes (large pan, 2 batches). Start at T minus 30 min.

YOU NEED
• Zucchini: 1000 g (about 3 large)
• Parmesan: 50 g
• Olive oil: 30 ml (2 tablespoons)
• Garlic: 10 g (2 cloves)
• Dried oregano: 1 teaspoon
• Chili flakes: 1 pinch
• Salt: 1 teaspoon
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Large pan, grater

GET READY
1. Wash the zucchini. Cut off both ends.
2. Cut the zucchini into rounds about 1 cm thick.
3. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
4. Grate the parmesan on the small holes of the grater.

COOK (2 batches)
5. Heat the pan on medium heat for 1 minute. Add 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of oil.
6. Add half of the garlic. Cook for 30 seconds, until fragrant.
7. Add half of the zucchini in one layer.
8. Cook for 10 minutes, turning the rounds every 2 to 3 minutes, until tender and a little golden.
9. Sprinkle 1/2 teaspoon of oregano, 1/2 teaspoon of salt, 1/4 teaspoon of pepper and a tiny pinch of chili flakes.
10. Move the zucchini to a warm platter. Cover with foil.
11. Repeat steps 5 to 10 with the second half.
12. Sprinkle the parmesan over all the zucchini.
✅ READY WHEN: the rounds are soft but still keep their shape, and are golden at the edges.
⚠️ Do not crowd the pan. Crowded zucchini steams and turns watery and soft.
⚠️ Add the salt only at the end of each batch. Early salt makes the zucchini watery.

🥦 OVEN-ROASTED CAULIFLOWER STEAKS WITH CHEESE AND BACON — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 60 minutes (oven, 2 baking trays, 1 pan). Start at T minus 65 min.

YOU NEED
• Cauliflower: 1600 g (2 large heads)
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 240 g
• Bacon: 240 g (about 8 slices)
• Olive oil: 60 ml (4 tablespoons)
• Paprika: 1 teaspoon
• Garlic: 5 g (1 clove)
• Mayonnaise: 100 g
• Lime juice: 1 tablespoon
• Salt: 1 1/2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 3/4 teaspoon
• 2 baking trays, baking paper, large pan, sharp knife, brush or spoon

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 220°C. Put the shelves in the top and the middle.
2. Cover the 2 baking trays with baking paper.
3. Cut the leaves off the cauliflower. Trim the stem so the head can stand flat. Keep the core.
4. Stand a head on its stem. Cut it into slices about 2 cm thick ("steaks"). The 2 middle slices stay whole. Keep the loose florets too.
5. Peel the garlic and chop it finely. Grate the cheese.
6. Mix the olive oil, the paprika, the salt and the pepper in a small bowl.
7. Mix the dressing in another bowl: mayonnaise, garlic and lime juice. Cover and keep in the fridge.

ROAST
8. Brush both sides of each steak with the oil mix. Toss the loose florets in the rest of the oil mix.
9. Lay the steaks and florets on the trays in one layer.
10. Roast for 25 to 30 minutes. Turn the steaks over after 15 minutes. Swap the trays at the same time.
11. When tender and golden brown in spots, sprinkle the cheese over them.
12. Roast for 3 to 4 more minutes, until the cheese is melted.

BACON (while the cauliflower roasts)
13. Put the bacon in a cold pan on medium heat.
14. Fry for 8 minutes, turning once, until crispy.
15. Move the bacon to paper towel. Crumble it when cool.

SERVE
16. Move the steaks and florets to a platter.
17. Drizzle the dressing over the top in zigzag lines. Scatter the bacon on top.
✅ READY WHEN: the cauliflower is soft when a knife goes in, golden brown at the edges, and the cheese is melted.
⚠️ Do not slice the cauliflower thinner than 2 cm. Thin slices fall apart.
⚠️ Make sure the slices are in one layer. Crowded slices steam instead of roasting.

🍜 SESAME PEANUT NOODLES (shirataki) — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 20 minutes (1 large pan). Start at T minus 25 min.

YOU NEED
• Shirataki noodles: 800 g
• Peanut butter (smooth, natural): 75 g (5 tablespoons)
• Toasted sesame oil: 75 ml (5 tablespoons)
• Tamari sauce: 30 ml (2 tablespoons)
• Vinegar: 30 ml (2 tablespoons)
• Garlic: 15 g (3 cloves)
• Fresh ginger: 10 g (2 teaspoons, grated)
• Chili flakes: 1/2 teaspoon
• Butter: 15 g
• Red onion: 30 g
• Sesame seeds: 2 tablespoons
• Measuring jug, whisk, colander, large pan

GET READY
1. Peel the garlic and grate it. Peel the ginger and grate it.
2. Put the peanut butter, the sesame oil, the tamari, the vinegar, the garlic, the ginger and the chili flakes in a jug.
3. Whisk until smooth.
4. Peel the red onion and cut it into thin half-rings.
5. Open the noodle packs over the sink and pour them into a colander.
6. Rinse under cold water for 2 minutes. Shake off the water.

COOK
7. Heat the large pan on medium heat for 2 minutes. Add the butter.
8. Add the noodles. Fry for 3 minutes, stirring, until they squeak and the pan is dry.
9. Pour the sauce into the pan.
10. Stir for 1 to 2 minutes until every noodle is coated.
11. Take the pan off the heat.
12. Put the noodles in a bowl. Sprinkle the onion and the sesame seeds on top.
✅ READY WHEN: the noodles are hot and evenly coated in a brown creamy sauce.
⚠️ Do not add salt. Tamari is already salty.
⚠️ If the pan is wet in step 8, cook longer before you add the sauce.
