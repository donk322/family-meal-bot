🥗 COBB SALAD — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Chicken, bacon, egg, avocado and tomato on lettuce with a creamy dressing.
⏱ 50 minutes (pot, pan). Start at T minus 55 min.

YOU NEED
• Chicken breast: 600 g
• Bacon: 200 g (about 7 slices)
• Eggs: 8
• Avocados: 400 g (about 3)
• Tomatoes: 400 g
• Romaine lettuce: 600 g (2 heads)
• Red onion: 50 g
• Dressing: mayonnaise 120 g, olive oil 45 ml, lime juice 2 tablespoons, mustard 1 tablespoon
• Salt: 1 1/2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Pot, pan, thermometer, large platter, small bowl

COOK THE CHICKEN AND EGGS
1. Put the chicken in a pot and cover it with cold water. Add 1 teaspoon of salt.
2. Bring to a boil, then turn the heat to low so it only bubbles gently. Cook for 15 to 20 minutes.
3. Push the thermometer into the thickest piece. It must read at least 74°C.
4. Take the chicken out and let it cool for 15 minutes.
5. Put the eggs in another pot, cover with cold water by 2 cm, and bring to a boil.
6. As soon as it boils, turn the heat off, put the lid on and wait 11 minutes.
7. Pour out the hot water and fill the pot with cold water for 10 minutes. Peel the eggs.

BACON
8. Put the bacon in a cold pan on medium heat. Fry for 8 minutes, turning once, until crispy.
9. Move the bacon to paper towel. Crumble it when cool.

GET READY
10. Cut the chicken into cubes of 1.5 cm.
11. Cut the eggs into quarters.
12. Cut the avocados in half, take out the stones and cut the flesh into cubes of 2 cm. Put 1 tablespoon of the lime juice on them.
13. Cut the tomatoes into cubes of 2 cm. Peel the red onion and cut it into very thin half-rings.
14. Wash the lettuce. Dry it well. Tear it into pieces of about 5 cm.
15. Make the dressing: whisk the mayonnaise, the oil, the other tablespoon of lime juice, the mustard, 1/2 teaspoon of salt and the pepper in a small bowl.

ASSEMBLE
16. Spread the lettuce over a large platter.
17. Lay the chicken, the bacon, the eggs, the avocado, the tomatoes and the onion in separate stripes across the lettuce.
18. Put the dressing in a small bowl next to the platter.
✅ READY WHEN: each ingredient is in its own neat stripe and the avocado is still green.
⚠️ Cut the avocado last. It turns brown within 30 minutes.
⚠️ Dry the lettuce well. Wet lettuce makes the dressing watery.

🥗 GREEK SALAD — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 15 minutes. Start at T minus 20 min.

YOU NEED
• Cucumbers: 600 g (about 2 long cucumbers)
• Tomatoes: 600 g (about 5 medium)
• Red onion: 100 g (1 small)
• Feta: 240 g
• Pitted black olives: 120 g
• Olive oil: 90 ml (6 tablespoons)
• Vinegar: 30 ml (2 tablespoons)
• Dried oregano: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Large bowl, sharp knife

STEPS
1. Wash the cucumbers and the tomatoes.
2. Cut each cucumber in half lengthwise. Cut the halves into half-moon slices about 1 cm thick.
3. Cut each tomato into 8 wedges.
4. Peel the red onion and cut it into thin half-rings.
5. Cut the feta into cubes of 2 cm.
6. Put the cucumbers, tomatoes, onion and olives in a large bowl.
7. Mix the oil, the vinegar, the oregano and the pepper in a cup.
8. Pour the dressing over the vegetables and toss gently, 4 or 5 turns.
9. Put the feta on top. Do not mix it in.
✅ READY WHEN: the vegetables are coated in oil and the feta is on top in whole cubes.
⚠️ Do not add salt. Feta and olives are already salty.
⚠️ Make it no more than 20 minutes before serving. The tomatoes make it watery.

🥗 CHICKEN CAESAR SALAD WITH GLUTEN-FREE CROUTONS — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 50 minutes (pot, oven). Start at T minus 55 min.

YOU NEED
• Chicken breast: 600 g
• Romaine lettuce: 600 g (2 heads)
• Parmesan: 100 g
• Gluten-free bread: 8 slices
• Olive oil: 30 ml (2 tablespoons, for the croutons)
• Dressing: mayonnaise 150 g, parmesan 30 g (from the 100 g), garlic 5 g (1 clove), lime juice 2 tablespoons, mustard 1 teaspoon, black pepper 1/2 teaspoon
• Salt: 1 teaspoon for the chicken
• Pot, baking tray, thermometer, grater, large bowl, small bowl

COOK THE CHICKEN
1. Put the chicken in a pot and cover it with cold water. Add 1 teaspoon of salt.
2. Bring to a boil, then turn the heat to low so it only bubbles gently. Cook for 15 to 20 minutes.
3. Push the thermometer into the thickest piece. It must read at least 74°C.
4. Take the chicken out and let it cool for 15 minutes.

CROUTONS
5. Turn the oven on to 180°C.
6. Cut the bread into cubes of 2 cm.
7. Put the cubes on a baking tray. Pour the oil over them and toss.
8. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring once, until golden and hard.
9. Let the croutons cool on the tray.

DRESSING AND SALAD
10. Grate 30 g of parmesan on the small holes. Peel the garlic and crush it very finely.
11. Whisk the mayonnaise, the grated parmesan, the garlic, the lime juice, the mustard and the pepper in a small bowl.
12. Wash the lettuce. Dry it well. Tear it into pieces of about 5 cm. Put it in a large bowl.
13. Cut the chicken into thin slices or cubes of 1.5 cm.
14. With a vegetable peeler, shave the rest of the parmesan (70 g) into thin curls.

ASSEMBLE
15. Pour the dressing over the lettuce and toss until every leaf is coated.
16. Put the salad on a large platter. Lay the chicken on top.
17. Scatter the croutons and the parmesan curls on top.
✅ READY WHEN: the lettuce is lightly coated in dressing and the croutons are still crunchy.
⚠️ Add the croutons last. They go soft in 10 minutes.
⚠️ Do not use a bought dressing. It often contains gluten.

🧀 CHEESE SALAD WITH EGGS AND GARLIC — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 30 minutes, then 20 minutes in the fridge. Start at T minus 55 min.

YOU NEED
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 320 g
• Eggs: 8
• Mayonnaise: 160 g (10 tablespoons)
• Garlic: 10 g (2 cloves)
• Dill: 5 g
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Salt: 1/2 teaspoon
• Pot, grater, bowl

STEPS
1. Put the eggs in a pot and cover with cold water by 2 cm. Bring to a boil.
2. As soon as it boils, turn the heat off, put the lid on and wait 11 minutes.
3. Pour out the hot water and fill the pot with cold water for 10 minutes. Peel the eggs.
4. Grate the cheese on the large holes of the grater into a bowl.
5. Grate the eggs on the large holes into the same bowl.
6. Peel the garlic, crush it with the flat side of a knife and chop it as fine as you can.
7. Chop the dill finely.
8. Add the garlic, the dill, the mayonnaise, the pepper and the salt to the bowl.
9. Mix with a spoon until everything is coated.
10. Cover and put in the fridge for 20 minutes.
✅ READY WHEN: the salad is pale yellow and holds together when you press it with a spoon.
⚠️ Add the garlic carefully. Raw garlic is strong.
⚠️ Taste it before you serve. If it is too thick, add 1 tablespoon of mayonnaise.

🥗 MEAT SALAD WITH AVOCADO (no potato) — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A salad like the classic Russian one, but with avocado instead of potato.
⏱ 60 minutes (2 pots). Start at T minus 65 min.

YOU NEED
• Chicken breast: 600 g
• Eggs: 6
• Carrots: 150 g
• Pickled cucumbers: 150 g
• Avocados: 300 g (about 2)
• Red onion: 50 g
• Mayonnaise: 200 g (13 tablespoons)
• Lime juice: 1 tablespoon
• Dill: 5 g
• Salt: 1 1/2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• 2 pots, thermometer, big bowl, sharp knife

COOK
1. Put the chicken in a pot and cover it with cold water. Add 1 teaspoon of salt.
2. Bring to a boil, then turn the heat to low. Cook for 15 to 20 minutes.
3. Push the thermometer into the thickest piece. It must read at least 74°C.
4. Take the chicken out and let it cool for 15 minutes.
5. Put the whole carrots and the eggs in the second pot. Cover with cold water and bring to a boil.
6. After 11 minutes, take the eggs out into cold water. Cook the carrots 10 more minutes until soft.
7. Peel the eggs. Peel the carrots. Let everything cool for 15 minutes.

GET READY
8. Cut the chicken into cubes of 1 cm.
9. Cut the eggs, the carrots and the pickled cucumbers into cubes of 1 cm.
10. Peel the red onion and cut it into very small cubes. Chop the dill finely.
11. Cut the avocados in half, take out the stones and cut the flesh into cubes of 1 cm. Mix with the lime juice.

ASSEMBLE
12. Put the chicken, eggs, carrots, cucumbers, onion and dill in a big bowl.
13. Add the mayonnaise, 1/2 teaspoon of salt and the pepper. Mix gently.
14. Fold in the avocado at the end, with 3 or 4 turns.
15. Cover and put in the fridge for 15 minutes.
✅ READY WHEN: everything is cut to the same small size and covered in mayonnaise, and the avocado is still in pieces.
⚠️ Cool the chicken, eggs and carrots before mixing. Warm food makes the mayonnaise runny.
⚠️ Fold the avocado in last, gently. It turns to mush if you stir it.

🥗 BEETROOT SALAD WITH MAYONNAISE AND GARLIC — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ About 100 minutes, mostly waiting. Start at T minus 130 min.

YOU NEED
• Beetroot: 800 g (about 4 medium)
• Mayonnaise: 120 g (8 tablespoons)
• Garlic: 5 g (1 medium clove)
• Salt: 1/2 teaspoon
• Large pot, grater, bowl, gloves (optional)

STEPS
1. Wash the beetroot well under cold water. Do NOT peel it and do NOT cut off the tails.
2. Put the beetroot in a large pot and cover with cold water, 3 cm above them.
3. Put the pot on high heat and bring to a boil.
4. Turn the heat to medium-low so it bubbles gently, and put the lid on.
5. Cook for 50 to 60 minutes.
6. Push a knife into the biggest beetroot. If it goes in easily, they are ready. If it is hard, cook 10 more minutes and test again.
7. Pour out the hot water and fill the pot with cold water. Leave for 10 minutes.
8. Rub the skin off with your fingers. It slides off easily.
9. Grate the beetroot on the large holes of the grater into a bowl.
10. Peel the garlic, crush it with the flat side of a knife, then chop it as fine as you can.
11. Add the garlic, the mayonnaise and the salt to the beetroot.
12. Mix with a spoon until the colour is even.
13. Cover and put in the fridge for at least 20 minutes.
✅ READY WHEN: deep red and soft, with no hard spots, and the mayonnaise is mixed all the way through.
⚠️ Beetroot stains hands, boards and clothes. Wear gloves or wash your hands right after.

🍤 PRAWN AND AVOCADO SALAD — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 30 minutes (pot). Start at T minus 35 min.

YOU NEED
• Prawns: 800 g
• Avocados: 500 g (about 3 or 4)
• Cucumber: 300 g
• Red onion: 50 g
• Lime juice: 4 tablespoons
• Mayonnaise: 100 g
• Garlic: 5 g (1 clove)
• Salt: 1 teaspoon for the water + 1/2 teaspoon for the salad
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Large pot, bowl of ice water, colander, large bowl

COOK THE PRAWNS
1. If the prawns are frozen, thaw them in the fridge overnight.
2. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil. Add 1 teaspoon of salt.
3. Put the prawns in the water. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, until they are pink and curled into a loose "C".
4. Pour them into a colander. Put them in the bowl of ice water for 2 minutes.
5. Drain them well and pat them dry. Peel them if they have shells.

GET READY
6. Cut the avocados in half, take out the stones and cut the flesh into cubes of 2 cm.
7. Put the avocado in a large bowl with 2 tablespoons of lime juice.
8. Cut the cucumber into cubes of 1 cm. Peel the red onion and cut it into very thin half-rings.
9. Make the dressing: peel the garlic and chop it very fine. Mix it with the mayonnaise, the other 2 tablespoons of lime juice, 1/2 teaspoon of salt and the pepper.

ASSEMBLE
10. Add the prawns, the cucumber and the onion to the avocado.
11. Pour the dressing over the top.
12. Mix gently, 3 or 4 turns. Do not mash the avocado.
✅ READY WHEN: the prawns are pink and firm, the avocado is in pieces, and everything is lightly coated.
⚠️ Prawns need only 2 to 3 minutes. Longer makes them rubbery.
⚠️ Make this salad last, just before serving. Avocado turns brown in 30 minutes.
