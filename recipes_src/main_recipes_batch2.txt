🍗 CRISPY OVEN CHICKEN WINGS WITH SMOKY DIP — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 60 minutes (oven, 2 baking trays). Start at T minus 65 min.

YOU NEED
• Chicken wings (split into pieces): 1500 g
• Olive oil: 30 ml (2 tablespoons)
• Arrowroot: 1 1/2 tablespoons (this makes the skin crisp)
• Paprika: 2 teaspoons
• Salt: 1 teaspoon
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• For the dip: mayonnaise 100 g (6 tablespoons), paprika 1 teaspoon, chili flakes 1/4 teaspoon, lime juice 1 tablespoon
• 2 baking trays, foil, paper towels, thermometer, big bowl

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 220°C. Put the oven shelves in the top and the middle.
2. Cover the 2 baking trays with foil.
3. Pat every wing piece completely dry with paper towel. Dry wings get crispy, wet wings do not.
4. Put the wings in a big bowl.
5. Add the oil and toss until every piece is coated.
6. Mix the arrowroot, the paprika, the salt and the pepper in a small bowl.
7. Sprinkle it over the wings and toss until they are evenly coated.
8. Mix the dip in a small bowl: mayonnaise, paprika, chili flakes and lime juice. Cover it and put it in the fridge.

BAKE
9. Lay the wings on the trays in one layer, skin side up, with space between them.
10. Bake for 25 minutes.
11. Swap the trays' places. Turn each wing over with tongs.
12. Bake for 15 to 20 more minutes, until deep golden and crisp.
13. Push the thermometer into the thickest wing, not touching the bone. It must read at least 80°C.
14. Take the trays out and let the wings rest for 5 minutes.
15. Put the wings on a platter. Put the dip in a small bowl next to them.
✅ READY WHEN: the skin is deep golden and crisp, the thermometer shows 80°C or more, and the juice is clear.
⚠️ Do not crowd the trays. Crowded wings steam and stay soft.
⚠️ Wash your hands and the board after touching raw chicken.

🍝 CHEESE MEATBALLS IN TOMATO SAUCE — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
24 meatballs, 3 per portion.
⏱ 60 minutes (large pan, 2 batches). Start at T minus 65 min.

YOU NEED
• Lean ground beef: 1200 g
• Napoletana tomato sauce: 700 g
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 90 g
• Parmesan: 40 g
• Eggs: 2
• Onion: 150 g
• Garlic: 15 g (3 cloves: 1 for the meatballs, 2 for the sauce)
• Olive oil: 45 ml (3 tablespoons)
• Dried oregano: 1 1/2 teaspoons
• Salt: 2 1/4 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 1/4 teaspoons
• Large deep pan with a lid, big bowl, thermometer, paper towels

GET READY
1. Grate the cheddar and the parmesan on the small holes of the grater.
2. Peel the garlic. Chop 1 clove finely for the meatballs. Chop 2 cloves for the sauce.
3. Peel the onion and cut it into small cubes.
4. Put the beef, the 1 clove of garlic, the cheeses, the eggs, 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt and 3/4 teaspoon of pepper in a big bowl.
5. Mix with your hands for 1 minute, until just combined. Do not squeeze it hard.
6. Wet your hands with water. Divide the meat into 24 equal balls (about 50 g each).

BROWN THE MEATBALLS (2 batches of 12)
7. Heat the pan on medium heat. Add 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of oil.
8. Put 12 meatballs in the pan with space between them.
9. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes, turning with tongs every 2 minutes, until golden on all sides.
10. Move them to a plate lined with paper towel.
11. Wipe the pan. Repeat steps 7 to 10 with the second 12 meatballs.

SAUCE
12. In the same pan, heat the last tablespoon (15 ml) of oil on medium heat.
13. Add the onion. Cook for 5 minutes until soft.
14. Add the 2 cloves of garlic. Stir for 1 minute.
15. Add the tomato sauce, the oregano, 3/4 teaspoon of salt and 1/2 teaspoon of pepper.
16. Put all the meatballs back in the sauce.
17. Cover with the lid. Simmer on low heat for 15 minutes.
18. Push the thermometer into the middle of a meatball. It must read at least 74°C.
✅ READY WHEN: the sauce is thick, the meatballs are firm and brown, and the thermometer reads 74°C or more.
⚠️ Do not mix the meat too long. Overmixed meatballs are hard.
⚠️ If the sauce is thin after 15 minutes, take the lid off and simmer 5 more minutes.

🌮 CHEESE TACO CUPS WITH BEEF — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Crispy cheese cups filled with spiced beef. 2 cups per portion.
⏱ 70 minutes (oven, large pan). Start at T minus 75 min. This dish needs a muffin tin.

YOU NEED
• Lean ground beef: 1200 g
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 240 g
• Avocado: 250 g
• Tomatoes: 250 g
• Sour cream: 200 ml
• Onion: 100 g
• Garlic: 15 g (3 cloves)
• Paprika: 2 teaspoons
• Dried oregano: 1 teaspoon
• Vegetable oil: 15 ml (1 tablespoon)
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• Muffin tin (12 holes), baking paper, 2 baking trays, large pan, thermometer, spoon

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 190°C.
2. Cover the baking trays with baking paper.
3. Grate the cheese on the large holes of the grater.
4. Peel the onion and cut it into small cubes. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
5. Cut the avocado into cubes of 1 cm and the tomatoes into cubes of 1 cm. Keep them in two bowls.

MAKE THE CHEESE CUPS (16 cups, 8 per tray)
6. Put 8 small heaps of cheese on each tray (1 tablespoon, 15 g each), far apart, so they can spread.
7. Bake for 6 minutes, until the cheese bubbles and the edges turn golden.
8. Let the cheese cool on the tray for 1 minute until it can be lifted.
9. Turn the muffin tin upside down. Lift each cheese disc with a spatula and drape it over a hole in the tin.
10. Let the cups cool for 10 minutes until hard.
11. Bake the second tray the same way and shape the rest of the cups.

BEEF FILLING
12. Heat the oil in the large pan on medium heat.
13. Add the onion. Cook for 5 minutes until soft.
14. Add the garlic. Stir for 1 minute.
15. Add the beef. Break it into small pieces.
16. Add the paprika, the oregano, the salt and the pepper.
17. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring, until no pink is left.
18. Push the thermometer into the beef. It must read at least 74°C.
19. Tilt the pan and spoon out the fat into a bowl. Throw it in the bin, not down the sink.

SERVE
20. Put the 16 cups on a platter.
21. Fill each cup with beef just before serving.
22. Put the avocado, the tomatoes and the sour cream in three bowls next to the platter.
✅ READY WHEN: the cups are hard and crisp, golden, and hold the filling without breaking.
⚠️ Fill the cups only when you serve. They go soft in 10 minutes.
⚠️ If a cup is soft, bake it again on the tray for 1 minute.

🥩 BACON-WRAPPED MEATLOAF WITH CHEESE — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 90 minutes (large pan, oven). Start at T minus 95 min.

YOU NEED
• Lean ground beef: 1200 g
• Bacon: 180 g (6 thin slices)
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 120 g
• Parmesan: 25 g
• Eggs: 3
• Onion: 150 g
• Celery: 80 g (2 stalks)
• Garlic: 15 g (3 cloves)
• Tamari sauce: 15 ml (1 tablespoon)
• Olive oil: 15 ml (1 tablespoon)
• Dried oregano: 1 teaspoon
• Paprika: 1 teaspoon
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• Baking dish about 23 x 33 cm, large pan, big bowl, thermometer, foil

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 190°C. Rub the baking dish with a little oil.
2. Peel the onion and cut it into small cubes. Cut the celery into small cubes.
3. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
4. Grate the cheddar and the parmesan.

COOK THE VEGETABLES
5. Heat 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of oil in the pan on medium heat.
6. Add the onion and the celery. Cook for 5 minutes until soft.
7. Add the garlic, the oregano and the paprika. Stir for 1 minute.
8. Let the mixture cool for 10 minutes.

MIX AND SHAPE
9. Put the beef, the cooled vegetables, the cheeses, the eggs, the tamari, the salt and the pepper in a big bowl.
10. Mix with your hands for 1 minute, until just combined.
11. Shape the meat into one big loaf in the baking dish, about 8 cm high and 12 cm wide.
12. Lay the bacon slices across the loaf, side by side.

BAKE
13. Bake for 60 to 70 minutes.
14. If the bacon darkens too fast, cover the dish with foil.
15. Push the thermometer into the middle of the loaf. It must read at least 74°C.
16. Take the dish out. Let the loaf rest for 10 minutes.
17. Cut the loaf into 8 thick slices.
✅ READY WHEN: the bacon is crisp and deep golden, the thermometer reads 74°C or more, and the juice is clear with no pink.
⚠️ Do not cut the loaf before the 10 minutes rest. It falls apart.
⚠️ If a lot of fat gathers in the dish, spoon it out before slicing.

🥬 STUFFED CABBAGE ROLLS IN TOMATO SAUCE — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
16 rolls, 2 per portion.
⏱ 100 minutes (pot, large pan, oven). Start at T minus 105 min.

YOU NEED
• Cabbage: 1 large head (you need 16 whole leaves)
• Lean ground beef: 600 g
• Pork mince: 450 g
• Cauliflower: 150 g
• Red onion: 60 g
• Napoletana tomato sauce: 600 g
• Olive oil: 60 ml (4 tablespoons)
• Vinegar: 1 tablespoon
• Dried oregano: 1 teaspoon
• Chili flakes: 1/2 teaspoon
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• Large pot, large deep pan, baking dish about 23 x 33 cm, grater, thermometer, foil

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 180°C.
2. Cut the hard white core out of the cabbage. Peel off 16 whole leaves, without tearing them.
3. Grate the cauliflower on the large holes of the grater. This is the "rice".
4. Peel the onion and cut it into very small cubes.
5. Bring a large pot of water to a boil.

BOIL THE LEAVES
6. Using tongs, put 3 or 4 leaves in the boiling water for 1 minute, until soft.
7. Lift them out onto a plate. Repeat until all 16 leaves are soft.
8. With a knife, shave down the thick white rib at the bottom of each leaf.

SAUCE
9. Heat the oil in the large deep pan on medium heat.
10. Add the tomato sauce, the vinegar, the oregano and the chili flakes.
11. Add 1 teaspoon of salt and 1/2 teaspoon of pepper.
12. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cook for 15 minutes.

FILLING AND ROLLING
13. Put the beef, the pork, the cauliflower, the onion, 1 teaspoon of salt, 1/2 teaspoon of pepper and 4 tablespoons of the sauce in a big bowl.
14. Mix with your hands for 1 minute, until just combined.
15. Spread a thin layer of sauce on the bottom of the baking dish.
16. Lay one leaf flat, thick end toward you. Put about 3 tablespoons (60 g) of filling on the thick end.
17. Fold the thick end over the filling. Fold both sides in. Roll up tightly.
18. Put the roll in the dish, seam side down. Repeat for all 16 rolls, packing them close together.

BAKE
19. Pour the rest of the sauce over the rolls.
20. Cover the dish tightly with foil.
21. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes.
22. Push the thermometer into the middle of a roll. It must read at least 74°C.
23. Take the foil off and bake for 10 more minutes.
✅ READY WHEN: the cabbage is very soft, the sauce is bubbling, and the filling reads 74°C or more.
⚠️ Do not overfill the leaves. They split when you roll them.
⚠️ Keep the foil tight in step 20. The steam makes the rolls soft.
