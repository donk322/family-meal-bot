🍤 SHRIMP AND SHIRATAKI NOODLE STIR-FRY — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A fasting-day dish (no meat).
⏱ 30 minutes (1 large pan, 2 batches). Start at T minus 35 min.

YOU NEED
• Prawns: 800 g
• Shirataki noodles: 800 g
• Cabbage: 500 g
• Carrots: 100 g
• Red onion: 60 g
• Garlic: 20 g (4 cloves)
• Tamari sauce: 45 ml (3 tablespoons)
• Coconut aminos: 45 ml (3 tablespoons)
• Chicken stock cube: 1 (dissolved in 100 ml of hot water)
• Chili flakes: 1/2 teaspoon
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Vegetable oil: 30 ml (2 tablespoons)
• Large pan, colander, grater, tongs

GET READY
1. Open the noodle packs over the sink and pour them into a colander.
2. Rinse under cold water for 2 minutes. Shake off the water.
3. If the prawns are frozen, thaw them in the fridge overnight. Pat them dry with paper towel.
4. Peel the prawns if they have shells. Leave the tails on.
5. Cut the cabbage into thin strips about 5 mm wide.
6. Grate the carrots on the large holes of the grater.
7. Peel the garlic and chop it finely. Peel the onion and cut it into thin half-rings.
8. Dissolve the stock cube in 100 ml of hot water.
9. Mix the tamari and the coconut aminos in a small bowl.

COOK (2 batches, each with half of the ingredients)
10. Heat the pan on medium-high for 2 minutes. Add 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of oil.
11. Add half of the onion and half of the garlic. Stir for 1 minute.
12. Add half of the cabbage. Stir for 2 minutes.
13. Pour in half of the stock. Cook for 1 minute until the cabbage wilts.
14. Add half of the carrots and stir for 30 seconds.
15. Add half of the prawns. Stir for 2 to 3 minutes, until all the prawns are pink and curled.
16. Add half of the noodles and half of the tamari mix.
17. Add 1/4 teaspoon of chili flakes and 1/4 teaspoon of pepper. Stir for 1 minute.
18. Move everything to a warm bowl and cover with foil.
19. Wipe the pan. Repeat steps 10 to 18 with the second half.
20. Mix both batches together.
✅ READY WHEN: the prawns are pink and firm (not grey and not rubbery), the cabbage is soft, and the noodles are hot and brown from the sauce.
⚠️ Prawns cook in 2 to 3 minutes. Longer makes them rubbery.
⚠️ Do not add salt. Tamari and coconut aminos are salty.

🍝 CHICKEN TETRAZZINI WITH SHIRATAKI NOODLES — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Chicken and noodles in a creamy cheese sauce, baked.
⏱ 65 minutes (pot, 1 large pan, oven). Start at T minus 70 min.

YOU NEED
• Chicken breast: 600 g
• Shirataki noodles (fettuccine or angel hair): 700 g
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 170 g
• Milk: 360 ml
• Water: 180 ml
• Red onion: 100 g
• Bell pepper: 200 g
• Garlic: 10 g (2 cloves)
• Arrowroot: 1 1/2 tablespoons
• Olive oil: 30 ml (2 tablespoons)
• Salt: 1 teaspoon
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Baking dish about 23 x 33 cm, large pan, pot, colander, thermometer, kitchen scissors

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 200°C. Rub the baking dish with a little olive oil.
2. Rinse the noodles in a colander under cold water for 2 minutes. Squeeze out the water.
3. Cut the noodles 5 or 6 times with scissors into short pieces. Put them in a bowl.
4. Peel the onion and cut it into small cubes. Cut the pepper into cubes of about 1 cm.
5. Peel the garlic and chop it finely. Grate the cheese.

COOK THE CHICKEN
6. Put the chicken in a pot and cover it with cold water.
7. Add 1 teaspoon of salt. Bring to a boil, then turn the heat down so it only bubbles gently.
8. Cook for 15 to 20 minutes. Push the thermometer into the thickest piece. It must read at least 74°C.
9. Take the chicken out. Let it cool for 10 minutes.
10. Shred the chicken into thin pieces with two forks.

SAUCE
11. Heat the oil in the large pan on medium-high heat. Add the onion and fry for 2 minutes.
12. Add the pepper and garlic. Fry for 3 minutes, stirring.
13. In a cup, mix the arrowroot with 3 tablespoons of cold water until smooth.
14. Pour the milk and the 180 ml of water into the pan. Bring to a gentle simmer.
15. Stir in the arrowroot mix. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring, until the sauce is thick.
16. Add 120 g of the cheese. Stir until melted.
17. Add the pepper. Stir in the noodles and the chicken.

BAKE
18. Pour everything into the baking dish. Sprinkle 50 g of cheese on top.
19. Bake for 15 minutes, until golden and bubbling.
✅ READY WHEN: the top is golden, the sauce bubbles at the edges, and the noodles are hot.
⚠️ The noodles release water. If the sauce is thin, bake 5 more minutes.
⚠️ Do not boil the sauce hard after adding the arrowroot. It turns slimy.

🫑 BACON AND BEEF STUFFED PEPPERS — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 75 minutes (large pot, oven). Start at T minus 80 min.

YOU NEED
• Bell peppers: 8 (about 1200 g)
• Lean ground beef: 1200 g
• Bacon: 200 g (about 6 slices)
• Napoletana tomato sauce: 600 g
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 240 g
• Onion: 150 g
• Mushroom stock cube: 1
• Paprika: 2 teaspoons
• Dried oregano: 2 teaspoons
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• Large pot, 2 baking dishes, thermometer, paper towels

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 180°C.
2. Cut each pepper in half from top to bottom. Take out the seeds. Keep the stalks on.
3. Cut the bacon into strips of 1 cm. Peel the onion and cut it into small cubes.
4. Grate the cheese. Dissolve the stock cube in 300 ml of hot water.

COOK THE FILLING
5. Put the bacon in the large pot on medium heat. Fry for 8 minutes until crispy.
6. Move the bacon to paper towel.
7. Add the onion to the pot. Cook for 5 minutes until soft.
8. Add the beef. Break it into small pieces with a spoon.
9. Cook for 7 minutes, until no pink is left.
10. Add the paprika, oregano, salt and pepper. Stir for 1 minute.
11. Add the tomato sauce and the stock.
12. Bring to a boil, then turn the heat to low. Simmer for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until slightly thick.

FILL AND BAKE
13. Put the 16 pepper halves in the baking dishes, cut side up.
14. Fill each half with the meat mixture.
15. Sprinkle the cheese on top, then the bacon.
16. Bake for 30 minutes, until the cheese is melted and the peppers are soft.
17. Push the thermometer into the middle of a filling. It must read at least 74°C.
✅ READY WHEN: the cheese is golden and bubbling, the peppers are soft when pressed with a fork, and the filling reads 74°C or more.
⚠️ Fill the peppers in the dish, not in your hand. They are heavy and drip.
⚠️ If the peppers are still hard after 30 minutes, bake 10 more minutes.

🍄 BEEF STROGANOFF WITH MUSHROOMS — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Ground beef and mushrooms in a creamy sauce.
⏱ 45 minutes (large pan, 3 batches of mushrooms). Start at T minus 50 min.

YOU NEED
• Lean ground beef: 1200 g
• Mushrooms: 1000 g
• Sour cream: 400 ml
• Butter: 60 g
• Garlic: 10 g (2 cloves)
• Mushroom stock cube: 1
• Tamari sauce: 2 teaspoons
• Mustard: 2 teaspoons
• Paprika: 1 teaspoon
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• Large deep pan, spatula, bowls

GET READY
1. Wipe the mushrooms with a damp paper towel. Cut them into slices about 5 mm thick.
2. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
3. Dissolve the stock cube in 400 ml of hot water.
4. Mix the sour cream, tamari, mustard and paprika in a bowl.

COOK THE BEEF
5. Heat the pan on medium-high for 2 minutes. Melt 20 g of butter.
6. Add the beef and sprinkle 1 teaspoon of salt and 1/2 teaspoon of pepper.
7. Break the beef into small pieces. Cook for 7 minutes, stirring, until no pink is left.
8. Move the beef to a bowl. Wipe the pan.

COOK THE MUSHROOMS (3 batches)
9. Melt 13 g of butter in the pan on medium-high heat.
10. Add one third of the mushrooms in one layer.
11. Leave them 3 minutes without stirring. Stir, then cook 4 more minutes until golden and dry.
12. Move them to a bowl. Repeat steps 9 to 11 two more times.
13. Put all the mushrooms back in the pan. Add the garlic. Stir for 1 minute.
14. Add 1 teaspoon of salt, 1/2 teaspoon of pepper and the stock. Bring to a simmer.
15. Stir in the sour cream mix. Heat for 2 minutes. Do not let it boil.
16. Add the beef back. Stir for 2 minutes.
✅ READY WHEN: the sauce is thick and pale brown, the mushrooms are soft, and everything is hot but not boiling.
⚠️ Boiling splits the sour cream. Keep the heat low after step 15.
⚠️ Do not crowd the mushrooms. Crowded mushrooms steam and turn grey.

🍗 PARMESAN-CRUSTED CHICKEN WITH TOMATO AND MOZZARELLA — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Chicken cutlets in a cheese crust (no flour), topped with tomato sauce and cheese.
⏱ 60 minutes (2 pans, oven). Start at T minus 65 min.

YOU NEED
• Chicken breast: 1200 g (6 breasts)
• Eggs: 4
• Parmesan: 340 g
• Mozzarella: 340 g
• Napoletana tomato sauce: 380 g
• Vegetable oil: 100 ml
• Dried oregano: 2 teaspoons
• Paprika: 1 teaspoon
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• 2 baking trays, 2 large pans, 3 shallow bowls, tongs, thermometer, sharp knife

GET READY
1. Put the oven shelf in the middle. Turn the oven on to 200°C.
2. Grate the parmesan on the small holes of the grater. Grate the mozzarella on the large holes.
3. Lay a chicken breast flat. Hold the palm of your hand on top.
4. Cut it in half through the middle, parallel to the board, to make 2 thin cutlets.
5. Repeat with all 6 breasts. You have 12 cutlets.
6. Sprinkle the cutlets with the salt and pepper on both sides.
7. Crack the eggs in a bowl and beat with a fork.
8. Mix the parmesan, oregano and paprika in a second bowl.

CRUMB
9. Dip one cutlet in the egg, turn it to coat both sides.
10. Press it into the parmesan mix on both sides, pressing hard so the cheese sticks.
11. Put it on a plate. Repeat with all the cutlets.

FRY (2 pans at the same time, in batches)
12. Heat both pans on medium heat. Add 2 tablespoons (30 ml) of oil to each.
13. Put 3 or 4 cutlets in each pan.
14. Fry for 2 to 3 minutes on the first side, until golden.
15. Turn and fry 2 to 3 minutes more.
16. Move the cutlets to a baking tray. Add oil to the pan and repeat until all are fried.

BAKE
17. Spoon 1 tablespoon of tomato sauce on each cutlet.
18. Put the mozzarella on top.
19. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbling.
20. Push the thermometer into the thickest cutlet. It must read at least 74°C.
✅ READY WHEN: the crust is golden, the cheese is melted and bubbling, and the chicken reads 74°C or more.
⚠️ Press the parmesan firmly on the chicken, or the crust falls off in the pan.
⚠️ Keep the heat on medium. Parmesan burns fast and tastes bitter.
