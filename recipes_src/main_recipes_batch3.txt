🥩 PORK CHOPS IN CREAMY MUSHROOM AND PARMESAN SAUCE — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 55 minutes (2 large pans). Start at T minus 60 min.

YOU NEED
• Boneless pork chops: 1400 g (8 chops of 175 g)
• Mushrooms: 450 g
• Spinach: 200 g
• Cream: 240 ml
• Parmesan: 110 g
• Garlic: 10 g (2 cloves)
• Olive oil: 60 ml (4 tablespoons)
• Dried oregano: 2 teaspoons
• Chili flakes: 1/2 teaspoon
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• 2 large pans with lids, tongs, thermometer, foil, plate

GET READY
1. Take the chops out of the fridge 20 minutes before cooking.
2. Wipe the mushrooms with a damp paper towel and cut them into slices about 5 mm thick.
3. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
4. Grate the parmesan on the small holes of the grater.
5. Pat the chops dry. Sprinkle them with 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt and 3/4 teaspoon of pepper on both sides.

COOK THE CHOPS (pan 1 and pan 2, 4 chops in each)
6. Heat both pans on medium-high for 2 minutes. Add 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of oil to each.
7. Put 4 chops in each pan with space between them.
8. Cook for 5 minutes on the first side without moving them.
9. Turn the chops with tongs. Cook for 5 minutes more.
10. Push the thermometer sideways into the thickest chop. It must read at least 70°C.
11. Move the chops to a plate and cover with foil. Keep the pans, with the juices.

SAUCE (in pan 1)
12. Pour the juices from pan 2 into pan 1. Wipe pan 2 and put it away.
13. Add 2 tablespoons (30 ml) of oil to pan 1 on medium heat.
14. Add the mushrooms. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring, until soft and golden.
15. Add the garlic. Stir for 1 minute.
16. Add the cream, the parmesan, the oregano, the chili flakes, 1/2 teaspoon of salt and 1/4 teaspoon of pepper.
17. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cook for 3 minutes, stirring, until thick.
18. Add the spinach in handfuls. Stir for 2 minutes until it is wilted.
19. Put the chops back into the sauce. Cover the pan and heat for 5 minutes on low.
✅ READY WHEN: the chops read 70°C or more, they are golden with a little pink-white juice, and the sauce is thick and creamy.
⚠️ Do not boil the cream sauce hard. It can split.
⚠️ Do not cook the chops past 75°C. Pork turns dry and tough.

🍲 BEEF AND MUSHROOM STEW — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A slow dish. It cooks gently for 1.5 hours.
⏱ About 2 hours 15 minutes (large pot with a lid). Start at T minus 140 min.

YOU NEED
• Beef (stewing pieces): 1200 g
• Mushrooms: 450 g
• Onion: 100 g
• Carrots: 100 g
• Celery: 80 g (2 stalks)
• Garlic: 15 g (3 cloves)
• Tomato paste: 30 g (2 tablespoons)
• Mushroom stock cubes: 2
• Water: 1800 ml
• Olive oil: 45 ml (3 tablespoons)
• Salt: 1 1/2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• Large heavy pot with a lid, slotted spoon, plate

GET READY
1. Pat the beef dry with paper towel. Cut it into cubes of about 3 cm.
2. Sprinkle the beef with 1 teaspoon of salt and the pepper.
3. Cut the mushrooms into quarters.
4. Peel the onion and cut it into small cubes. Peel the carrots and cut them into rounds of 1 cm.
5. Cut the celery into slices of 1 cm. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
6. Dissolve the 2 stock cubes in 1800 ml of hot water.

BROWN THE BEEF (3 batches)
7. Heat 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of oil in the pot on medium-high heat.
8. Put one third of the beef in the pot in one layer.
9. Cook for 3 minutes on each side until golden brown. Do not crowd the pot.
10. Move the beef to a plate. Repeat steps 7 to 9 with the other two thirds.

VEGETABLES
11. Add the mushrooms to the same pot. Cook for 5 minutes until golden.
12. Add the onion, carrots and celery. Cook for 5 minutes until soft.
13. Add the garlic. Stir for 1 minute.
14. Add the tomato paste. Stir for 1 minute until the vegetables are coated.

STEW
15. Add the beef and the stock to the pot. Add 1/2 teaspoon of salt.
16. Bring to a boil, then turn the heat to low so it only bubbles gently.
17. Put the lid on, leaving a small gap.
18. Cook for 1 1/2 hours.
19. Test a piece of beef with a fork.
✅ READY WHEN: a fork goes in easily and the beef nearly falls apart, and the sauce is thick enough to coat a spoon.
20. If the beef is still hard, cook 20 more minutes and test again.
⚠️ Do not boil the stew hard. It makes the beef tough.
⚠️ If the stew is too thin at the end, take the lid off and simmer 15 more minutes.

🍗 BACON CHICKEN THIGHS IN GARLIC CREAM SAUCE — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 70 minutes (2 large pans with lids). Start at T minus 75 min.

YOU NEED
• Chicken thighs (bone-in, with skin): 1400 g (about 8 thighs)
• Bacon: 150 g (about 5 slices)
• Mushrooms: 450 g
• Cream: 360 ml
• Red onion: 100 g
• Parmesan: 70 g
• Garlic: 15 g (3 cloves)
• Chicken stock cube: 1 (dissolved in 300 ml of hot water)
• Lime juice: 2 tablespoons
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• 2 large pans with lids, tongs, thermometer, paper towels

GET READY
1. Cut the bacon into strips of 1 cm.
2. Peel the red onion and cut it into small cubes. Cut the mushrooms into slices of 5 mm.
3. Peel the garlic and chop it finely. Grate the parmesan on the small holes.
4. Dissolve the stock cube in 300 ml of hot water. Squeeze 2 tablespoons of lime juice.
5. Pat the thighs dry. Sprinkle them with 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt and 3/4 teaspoon of pepper on both sides.

BACON AND CHICKEN
6. Put the bacon in pan 1 on medium heat. Fry for 8 minutes until crispy. Move the bacon to paper towel.
7. Leave about 2 tablespoons of bacon fat in pan 1. Pour the rest into a bowl.
8. Heat pan 2 on medium-high with 1 tablespoon of the bacon fat.
9. Put 4 thighs in each pan, skin side down. Cook for 5 minutes without moving, until golden.
10. Turn the thighs. Cook for 5 minutes more.
11. Move the thighs to a plate. Pour the juices from pan 2 into pan 1. Put pan 2 away.

SAUCE (in pan 1)
12. Add the onion to pan 1 on medium heat. Cook for 5 minutes until soft.
13. Add the mushrooms and 1/4 teaspoon of salt. Cook for 5 minutes until golden.
14. Add the garlic. Stir for 1 minute.
15. Pour in the stock, the cream, the parmesan and the lime juice.
16. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring.

FINISH
17. Put the thighs back in the pan, skin side up, so the skin stays above the sauce.
18. Cover the pan. Cook on low for 20 to 25 minutes.
19. Push the thermometer sideways into the thickest thigh, not touching the bone. It must read at least 74°C.
20. If it reads less, cook 5 more minutes and check again.
21. Scatter the bacon over the top just before serving.
✅ READY WHEN: the thermometer reads 74°C or more, the juice is clear, and the sauce is thick, creamy and pale brown.
⚠️ Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer. Hard boiling splits the cream.
⚠️ Add the lime juice in step 15 with the pan on low heat, not boiling.

🍖 SLOW-COOKED OXTAIL STEW — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A very long dish. The meat cooks for about 3 hours until it falls off the bone.
⏱ About 3 hours 40 minutes (large heavy pot with a lid). Start at T minus 220 min.

YOU NEED
• Oxtail (cut into pieces of 5 cm): 2000 g
• Onion: 300 g
• Garlic: 20 g (4 cloves)
• Tomato paste: 45 g (3 tablespoons)
• Arrowroot: 2 tablespoons
• Tamari sauce: 30 ml (2 tablespoons)
• Water: 2000 ml
• Vegetable oil: 30 ml (2 tablespoons)
• Chili flakes: 1/2 teaspoon
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• Large heavy pot with a lid, large bowl, slotted spoon

GET READY
1. Pat the oxtail pieces dry with paper towel.
2. Sprinkle them with 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt and the pepper.
3. Peel the onions and cut them into small cubes. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
4. Mix the arrowroot with 4 tablespoons of cold water in a cup until smooth.

BROWN THE OXTAIL (in batches)
5. Heat the oil in the pot on medium-high heat.
6. Put about a third of the oxtail pieces in the pot in one layer.
7. Cook for 3 minutes on each side until deep golden brown.
8. Move the pieces to a big bowl. Repeat until all the pieces are browned.

STEW
9. Add half of the onion to the pot. Cook for 6 minutes until soft.
10. Add the garlic and the tomato paste. Stir for 3 minutes until the paste darkens.
11. Stir the arrowroot mix again and pour it in. Stir for 1 minute.
12. Take the pot off the heat. Put the oxtail back in.
13. Add the water, the tamari, the chili flakes and the rest of the onion.
14. Put the pot back on the heat and bring to a boil. Then turn the heat to low so it only bubbles gently.
15. Put the lid on, leaving a small gap.
16. Cook for 3 hours. Stir gently every 30 minutes.
17. Test a piece of oxtail with a fork.
✅ READY WHEN: the meat pulls off the bone very easily and the sauce is thick and dark.
18. If the meat is still firm, cook 30 more minutes and test again.
19. Add 1/2 teaspoon of salt, stir and serve.
⚠️ Do not boil hard. A hard boil makes the meat tough.
⚠️ If the sauce gets too thick, add 100 ml of hot water and stir.
⚠️ The pot will be heavy. Use two hands and oven gloves.

🥩 GARLIC BUTTER BEEF STEAK SLICES — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Marinated beef slices seared fast and finished in garlic butter.
⏱ 60 minutes (large heavy pan). Start at T minus 65 min.

YOU NEED
• Beef steak: 1100 g
• Butter: 90 g
• Olive oil: 90 ml (6 tablespoons, for the marinade)
• Vinegar: 3 tablespoons
• Garlic: 20 g (4 cloves)
• Dried oregano: 2 teaspoons
• Chili flakes: 3/4 teaspoon
• Vegetable oil: 45 ml (1 tablespoon per batch)
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• Sharp knife, cutting board, big bowl, cling film, large heavy pan, tongs, foil, platter

GET READY
1. Take the steak out of the fridge 20 minutes before you start.
2. Pat the steak dry with paper towel. Look for the fine lines in the meat. You will cut ACROSS them.
3. Cut the steak across the lines into slices about 1 cm thick. Cut long slices in half.
4. Peel the garlic. Grate 1 clove into a big bowl. Crush and chop the other 3 cloves and keep them in a cup.
5. Add the olive oil, the vinegar, the oregano and the chili flakes to the bowl. Mix.
6. Add the slices and turn them until all are coated.
7. Cover the bowl and leave it at room temperature for 20 minutes.

COOK (batches of about 8 slices)
8. Lift the slices out of the marinade and pat them very dry with paper towel.
9. Sprinkle the slices with the salt and pepper on both sides.
10. Heat the pan on high for 3 minutes.
11. Flick a drop of water in. It must sizzle and disappear at once.
12. Add 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of vegetable oil and swirl.
13. Lay about 8 slices in the pan in one layer, not touching each other.
14. Fry for 1 1/2 to 2 minutes on the first side. Do not move them.
15. Turn each slice with tongs.
16. Fry for 1 to 1 1/2 minutes on the second side.
17. Move the slices to a plate and cover with foil.
18. Repeat steps 12 to 17 until all the slices are cooked.
19. After the last batch, turn the heat to medium-low.
20. Add the butter and the chopped garlic. Stir for 30 seconds until the butter melts and smells of garlic.
21. Put all the slices on a platter and pour the garlic butter over them.
✅ READY WHEN: the slices are brown outside and still pink inside. Cut the thickest slice open to check.
⚠️ Do not crowd the pan. Crowded slices steam and turn grey.
⚠️ Thin slices cook in about 3 minutes. Do not walk away from the pan.
⚠️ Garlic burns fast. Keep it in the pan for 30 seconds at most.
