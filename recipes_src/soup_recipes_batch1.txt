🥬 SHCHI (cabbage soup) WITH BEEF — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 100 minutes (large pot, 1 pan). Start at T minus 105 min.

YOU NEED
• Beef (stewing pieces): 600 g
• Cabbage: 700 g
• Onion: 150 g
• Carrots: 80 g
• Tomato paste: 20 g (1 heaped tablespoon)
• Butter: 60 g
• Garlic: 5 g (1 clove)
• Bay leaves: 2
• Water: 2800 ml
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Large pot, large pan, grater, slotted spoon, press or knife

GET READY
1. Cut the beef into pieces of about 3 cm.
2. Cut the cabbage into thin strips of about 5 mm.
3. Peel the onion and cut it into small cubes. Peel the carrots.
4. Grate the carrots on the small holes of the grater.
5. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.

BEEF BROTH
6. Put the beef in the large pot and pour in 2800 ml of cold water.
7. Put the pot on high heat and bring to a boil.
8. Skim the grey foam off the top with a slotted spoon. Keep skimming for about 5 minutes.
9. Turn the heat to low so it only bubbles gently. Put the lid on.
10. Cook for 1 hour, then test a piece of beef with a fork. It must be soft.
11. If it is still hard, cook 15 more minutes and test again.

VEGETABLES
12. Melt the butter in the large pan on medium heat.
13. Add the onion. Fry for 3 minutes until soft.
14. Add the carrots. Fry for 3 minutes.
15. Add the tomato paste. Stir for 2 minutes.

FINISH
16. Add the cabbage to the pot with the beef. Bring back to a boil. Cook for 5 minutes.
17. Add the fried vegetables to the pot. Stir. Cook for 3 minutes.
18. Add the garlic, the bay leaves, 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt and the pepper. Stir.
19. Turn off the heat. Cover the pot and leave it for 15 minutes.
20. Taste the soup. If it needs salt, add 1/2 teaspoon, stir for 1 minute and taste again.
✅ READY WHEN: the beef is soft, the cabbage is tender but not mushy, and the broth is golden-red.
⚠️ Take the bay leaves out before serving.
⚠️ Do not add all the salt at the start. The broth reduces.

🥬 LENTEN SHCHI WITH FRESH CABBAGE (Wednesday, no meat) — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 60 minutes (large pot). Start at T minus 65 min.

YOU NEED
• Cabbage: 450 g
• Potatoes: 700 g (about 5)
• Carrots: 200 g (about 2)
• Onion: 100 g (1 small)
• Butter: 30 g
• Garlic: 5 g (1 clove)
• Bay leaves: 2
• Black peppercorns: 5
• Water: 3000 ml
• Salt: 2 1/2 teaspoons
• Large pot, grater, slotted spoon

GET READY
1. Peel the onion and the potatoes. Cut the onion and the potatoes into cubes of about 1.5 cm.
2. Peel the carrots. Grate them on the small holes of the grater.
3. Cut the cabbage into thin strips of about 5 mm.
4. Peel the garlic, cut it in half and take out the green middle. Chop it finely.

COOK
5. Melt the butter in the large pot on medium heat.
6. Add the onion, the garlic and the carrots. Fry for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring, until golden.
7. Add the potatoes.
8. Pour in 3000 ml of water.
9. Bring to a boil. Add 2 teaspoons of salt, the peppercorns and the bay leaves.
10. Turn the heat to medium. Cook for 20 minutes.
11. Test a potato with a fork. It must be soft.
12. Add the cabbage.
13. Cook for 10 to 15 minutes more, until the cabbage is tender.
14. Taste the soup. If it needs salt, add 1/2 teaspoon, stir for 1 minute and taste again.
15. Take out the bay leaves.
✅ READY WHEN: the potatoes are soft, the cabbage is tender, and the broth is golden.
⚠️ Do not cook the cabbage longer than 15 minutes. It turns grey and bitter.
⚠️ Serve hot. You can serve it with a spoonful of sour cream.

🍅 TOMATO LENTIL SOUP WITH MUSHROOMS — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 80 minutes (large pot, 1 pan). Start at T minus 85 min.

YOU NEED
• Brown lentils: 320 g
• Tomatoes: 650 g
• Mushrooms: 200 g
• Carrots: 100 g
• Onion: 150 g
• Garlic: 15 g (3 cloves)
• Napoletana tomato sauce: 40 g (2 tablespoons)
• Butter: 40 g
• Dried oregano: 1 teaspoon
• Water: 2000 ml
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Large pot, large pan, grater, colander, slotted spoon

GET READY
1. Rinse the lentils in a colander under cold water 3 times.
2. Peel the onion and cut it into small cubes. Peel the garlic.
3. Peel the carrots. Grate them on the large holes.
4. Cut the mushrooms into small cubes of about 5 mm.
5. Cut each tomato into 4 pieces. Grate the pieces on the large holes of the grater, down to the skin. Throw the skin away.

LENTILS
6. Put the lentils in the large pot with 2000 ml of cold water.
7. Bring to a boil. Turn the heat to low and put the lid on.
8. Cook for 40 to 50 minutes, until the lentils are soft. Skim off any foam.

VEGETABLES
9. Melt the butter in the large pan on medium heat.
10. Add the onion and the whole garlic cloves. Fry for 3 minutes.
11. Add the carrots. Fry for 5 minutes.
12. Add the mushrooms. Fry for 5 to 7 minutes until the water has gone.
13. Add the grated tomatoes and the tomato sauce. Stir. Cook for 5 to 7 minutes until thick.

FINISH
14. Add the vegetables to the pot with the lentils. Stir.
15. Add the oregano, 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt and the pepper.
16. Cook for 10 minutes on low heat, stirring now and then.
17. Taste the soup. If it needs salt, add 1/2 teaspoon, stir for 1 minute and taste again.
✅ READY WHEN: the lentils are soft and the soup is thick and red-orange.
⚠️ Salt the lentils only at the end. Salt early makes them hard.
⚠️ If the soup is too thick, add 200 ml of hot water.

🍗 CHICKEN SOUP WITH SHIRATAKI NOODLES — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 55 minutes (large pot, 1 pan). Start at T minus 60 min.

YOU NEED
• Chicken breast: 450 g
• Shirataki noodles: 300 g
• Red bell pepper: 100 g
• Carrots: 80 g
• Onion: 60 g
• Butter: 40 g
• Water: 2800 ml
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Large pot, large pan, colander, thermometer, slotted spoon

GET READY
1. Rinse the chicken under cold water.
2. Peel the onion and cut it into small cubes. Peel the carrots.
3. Cut the carrots and the pepper into cubes of about 1 cm.
4. Rinse the noodles in a colander under cold water for 2 minutes. Cut them 3 times with scissors into shorter pieces.

BROTH
5. Put the chicken in the large pot and pour in 2800 ml of cold water.
6. Put the pot on high heat and bring to a boil. Add 1 1/2 teaspoons of salt.
7. Skim off the foam.
8. Turn the heat to low so it only bubbles gently. Cook for 25 minutes.
9. Push the thermometer into the thickest piece of chicken. It must read at least 74°C.
10. Take the chicken out. Let it cool for 10 minutes. Shred it into thin pieces with two forks.

VEGETABLES AND FINISH
11. Melt the butter in the large pan on medium heat.
12. Add the onion, the carrots and the pepper. Fry for 2 minutes, stirring.
13. Add the vegetables to the broth. Cook for 5 minutes.
14. Add the noodles and the shredded chicken.
15. Bring to a boil and cook for 2 minutes.
16. Add the pepper. Taste the soup. If it needs salt, add 1/2 teaspoon, stir for 1 minute and taste again.
✅ READY WHEN: the carrots are soft, the chicken is white all through, and the broth is clear and golden.
⚠️ Do not boil the broth hard. A hard boil makes it cloudy.
⚠️ Shirataki noodles do not need long cooking. 2 minutes is enough.
