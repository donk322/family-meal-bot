BREAKFAST RECIPES, BATCH 1 (5 dishes)
All quantities below are for 6 portions, as a reference size. In the bot the quantities will be recalculated for any number of portions.
T = the moment the family sits down to eat.

🥩 STEAK SANDWICH WITH BRIE, SOFT EGGS, ONIONS AND MUSHROOMS — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ About 60 minutes (pan 1, pan 2 and the oven grill). Start at T minus 65 min.

YOU NEED
• Beef steak: 750 g (about 125 g per sandwich)
• Gluten-free bread: 12 slices (2 per sandwich)
• Brie: 240 g (40 g per sandwich)
• Eggs: 12
• Onions: 450 g (about 3 large)
• Brown mushrooms: 450 g
• Butter: 65 g (45 g for the mushrooms, 20 g for the eggs)
• Milk: 60 ml (4 tablespoons)
• Garlic: 10 g (2 cloves)
• Olive oil: 30 ml (2 tablespoons), for the onions
• Vegetable oil: 45 ml (1 tablespoon per steak batch)
• Salt: 3 teaspoons in total (the steps say how much goes where)
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• 2 large pans, baking tray, foil, cling film, tongs, spatula, toaster

GET READY
1. Take the steak out of the fridge 20 minutes before you start cooking.
2. Peel the onions. Cut each onion in half, then into thin half-rings, about 3 mm.
3. Wipe the mushrooms with a damp paper towel. Cut them into slices about 5 mm thick.
4. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
5. Cut the brie into 6 equal pieces (40 g each). Keep them in the fridge.
6. Pat the steak dry with paper towel. Look for the fine lines in the meat. Cut ACROSS those lines into slices about 1 cm thick.
7. Lay the slices on a plate in one layer. Cover with cling film. Wash the knife, the board and your hands.
8. Crack the 12 eggs into a bowl. Add the milk, 1 teaspoon of salt and 1/4 teaspoon of pepper. Do not whisk yet.

CARAMELISE THE ONIONS (pan 1, 20 minutes)
9. Heat the olive oil in pan 1 on medium-low heat.
10. Add the onions and 1/4 teaspoon of salt.
11. Cook for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes.
✅ ONIONS ARE READY WHEN: they are very soft, deep golden brown and smell sweet. Do not hurry them. Pale onions are not sweet.
12. Move the onions to a bowl. Wipe pan 1 with paper towel.

FRY THE MUSHROOMS (pan 2, 10 minutes)
13. Heat pan 2 on medium-high for 2 minutes.
14. Add 45 g of butter and let it melt.
15. Add the mushrooms in one layer. If they do not fit, cook them in 2 batches.
16. Leave them for 3 minutes without stirring.
17. Stir. Cook 4 to 5 more minutes, until golden and no water is left in the pan.
18. Add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
19. Move the mushrooms to a bowl and stir in 1/2 teaspoon of salt. Wipe pan 2.

COOK THE STEAK (pan 2, 3 batches of about 7 slices)
20. Sprinkle the slices with 1 teaspoon of salt and 1/2 teaspoon of pepper, on both sides.
21. Heat pan 2 on high for 3 minutes.
22. Flick a drop of water in. It must sizzle and disappear at once.
23. Add 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of vegetable oil and swirl.
24. Lay about 7 slices in the pan in one layer, not touching each other.
25. Fry for 1 1/2 to 2 minutes on the first side. Do not move them.
26. Turn each slice with tongs.
27. Fry for 1 to 1 1/2 minutes on the second side.
28. Move the slices to a plate and cover with foil.
29. Repeat steps 23 to 28 for the second and third batches.
✅ STEAK IS READY WHEN: the slices are brown outside and still pink inside. Cut the thickest slice open to check.

SCRAMBLE THE EGGS (pan 1, 7 minutes)
30. Whisk the eggs with a fork for 20 seconds, until the colour is even.
31. Melt 20 g of butter in pan 1 on LOW heat.
32. Pour in the eggs and wait 20 seconds.
33. Stir slowly and all the time, pushing the egg from the edges to the middle, for 5 to 6 minutes.
✅ EGGS ARE READY WHEN: soft, creamy and still a little shiny.
34. Take the pan off the heat at once. Cover it with foil.

BUILD THE SANDWICHES
35. Turn the oven grill on to high. Put the oven shelf about 15 cm below the grill.
36. Toast all 12 slices of bread in the toaster.
37. Put 6 slices on a baking tray. Put one piece of brie on each.
38. Put the tray under the grill for 1 to 2 minutes, until the brie is soft and bubbling at the edges. Stay next to the oven.
39. Take the tray out. Put the steak slices on the melted brie, about 125 g on each.
40. Spoon the scrambled eggs on top, about 2 eggs on each.
41. Add some onions and mushrooms.
42. Put a second slice of toast on top. Press gently.
43. Cut each sandwich in half and put them on a warm platter. Serve hot.
⚠️ Brie melts fast. Never leave it under the grill alone.
⚠️ If your oven has no grill, put the tray in the oven at 200°C for 4 minutes instead.
⚠️ Do not stir the mushrooms in the first 3 minutes. They need time to brown.

🥩 BEEF AND PEPPER BREAKFAST SKILLET WITH CHEESE AND SMOKY MAYO — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱ 35 minutes (pan 1 and pan 2 at the same time). Start at T minus 40 min.

YOU NEED
• Lean ground beef: 1200 g (600 g per pan)
• Bell peppers: 450 g (about 3 medium)
• Eggs: 12
• Hard cheese (yellow cheddar block, NOT parmesan): 180 g
• Mayonnaise: 90 g (6 tablespoons)
• Lime juice: 2 teaspoons
• Paprika: 3 teaspoons
• Chili flakes: 1/4 teaspoon
• Salt: 2 teaspoons
• Black pepper: 1 teaspoon
• Vegetable oil: 30 ml (1 tablespoon per pan)
• 2 large pans with lids (or foil), 3 bowls, spatula, thermometer

GET READY
1. In a small bowl, mix the mayonnaise, 1 teaspoon of paprika, the chili flakes and the lime juice.
2. Cover the bowl and put it in the fridge.
3. Cut each pepper in half, take out the seeds and cut into cubes of about 1 cm.
4. Grate the cheese on the large holes of the grater.
5. Crack 6 eggs into a bowl. Do the same in a second bowl.
6. Whisk each bowl with a fork for 20 seconds, until the colour is even.
7. Mix the salt, the pepper and 2 teaspoons of paprika in a small bowl. Divide it in two.

COOK (2 pans at the same time)
8. Heat both large pans on medium-high for 2 minutes.
9. Add 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of oil to each pan.
10. Put half of the peppers (225 g) in each pan.
11. Fry for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring, until slightly soft.
12. Add 600 g of ground beef to each pan.
13. Break the beef into small pieces with the spatula.
14. Sprinkle half of the spice mix over the beef in each pan.
15. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring every minute, until the beef is brown all over with no pink left.
16. Push the thermometer into the beef. It must read at least 74°C.
17. If there is a lot of fat in the pan, spoon it into a bowl. Throw it in the bin, not down the sink.
18. Turn the heat to medium-low.
19. Pour 6 beaten eggs into each pan over the beef.
20. Stir gently for 2 to 3 minutes, until the eggs are just set but still a little shiny.
21. Sprinkle 90 g of cheese over each pan.
22. Cover each pan with a lid for 1 to 2 minutes, until the cheese melts.
✅ READY WHEN: no pink beef is left, the eggs are set but soft, and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
23. Spoon everything onto one big warm platter.
24. Drizzle the smoky mayo over the top in zigzag lines.
⚠️ Do not add the eggs until the beef has no pink left.
⚠️ If the cheese does not melt, give it 1 more minute under the lid.

🥦 CAULIFLOWER "FRIED RICE" WITH EGG, CARROT AND PEAS — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A light side dish. Serve it next to a main dish, not instead of one.
⏱ 30 minutes (1 large pan, 2 batches). Start at T minus 35 min.

YOU NEED
• Cauliflower: 1000 g (2 small heads)
• Eggs: 8
• Carrots: 200 g (about 2)
• Peas in pods (sugar snap peas): 120 g
• Red onion: 100 g (1 small)
• Garlic: 5 g (1 clove)
• Tamari sauce: 60 ml (4 tablespoons). This is the gluten-free soy sauce.
• Vegetable oil: 45 ml (3 tablespoons)
• Black pepper: 1/2 teaspoon
• Grater, large non-stick pan, clean kitchen towel, bowls

GET READY
1. Cut the cauliflower into florets. Cut off the hard stalks.
2. Grate the florets on the large holes of the grater into a big bowl. You want pieces the size of rice grains.
3. Put the grated cauliflower in a clean kitchen towel. Twist the towel and squeeze out as much water as you can.
4. Peel the carrots. Cut them into thin matchsticks, about 5 mm.
5. Cut the peas in half.
6. Peel the onion and cut it into thin half-rings. Peel the garlic and chop it finely.
7. Crack the eggs into a bowl. Whisk with a fork for 20 seconds.

COOK
8. Heat the large pan on medium-high for 2 minutes.
9. Add 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of oil and pour in the eggs.
10. Stir for 30 to 40 seconds, until scrambled in small pieces.
11. Move the eggs to a bowl.
12. Add 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of oil to the pan.
13. Add half of the onion and half of the carrots. Fry for 2 minutes, stirring.
14. Add half of the garlic and half of the peas. Stir for 30 seconds.
15. Add half of the cauliflower (500 g). Fry for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring often, until slightly soft and a little golden.
16. Pour in 2 tablespoons (30 ml) of tamari. Stir for 1 minute, until most of the liquid is gone.
17. Stir in half of the scrambled eggs.
18. Move everything to a warm bowl and cover with foil.
19. Repeat steps 12 to 17 with the second half of the ingredients and the last tablespoon of oil.
20. Mix both batches together. Add 1/2 teaspoon of black pepper and stir.
✅ READY WHEN: the cauliflower is soft but not mushy, and the dish looks dry, with golden flecks.
⚠️ Do not skip the squeezing in step 3. Wet cauliflower turns into a soggy porridge.
⚠️ Do not add salt. Tamari is already salty.
⚠️ If the pan looks wet after step 15, cook 1 more minute on high heat before adding the tamari.

🥚 FRENCH CREAMY EGGS — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2 eggs per portion. A light dish. Serve it next to a main dish.
⏱ 20 minutes. Start at T minus 20 min. Eggs are cooked LAST.

YOU NEED
• Eggs: 12
• Butter: 120 g, cold from the fridge
• Sour cream: 90 ml (6 tablespoons)
• Salt: 1 teaspoon (added at the very end)
• 1 large heavy saucepan, spatula, bowl, warm platter

GET READY
1. Cut the cold butter into cubes of about 1 cm.
2. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
3. Whisk gently with a fork for 15 seconds. Do not beat them hard. Do NOT add salt.
4. Rinse the platter with hot water and dry it.

COOK
5. Put the eggs and the butter cubes together into the COLD saucepan.
6. Put the pan on LOW heat (2 out of 10).
7. Stir all the time with the spatula, scraping the bottom and the sides.
8. Keep stirring for 8 to 10 minutes. The eggs thicken slowly.
9. When lumps start to form fast, or the bottom of the pan is very hot, lift the pan off the heat for 10 seconds and keep stirring. Then put it back.
10. Repeat step 9 as often as you need.
11. Take the pan off the heat when the eggs are creamy and thick but still a little runny, like thick porridge.
12. Stir in the sour cream. This stops the cooking.
13. Add the salt and stir.
14. Pour into the warm platter and serve at once.
✅ READY WHEN: velvety, glossy and loose, with small soft curds.
⚠️ Never use high heat. High heat makes scrambled eggs hard and dry.
⚠️ Slightly undercooked is correct. The eggs keep setting from their own heat.
⚠️ Serve at once. These eggs get firm within minutes.

🥚 DEVILED EGGS — serves 6
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
24 halves, 4 halves per portion. A cold dish. Make it before the hot dishes.
⏱ 50 minutes, mostly waiting. Start at T minus 55 min.

YOU NEED
• Eggs: 12
• Mayonnaise: 90 g (6 tablespoons)
• Mustard: 2 teaspoons
• Lime juice: 2 teaspoons
• Salt: 1/2 teaspoon
• Black pepper: 1/4 teaspoon
• Paprika: 1 teaspoon, for sprinkling
• Large pot with a lid, bowl, teaspoon, sharp knife, large platter, timer

BOIL
1. Put the eggs in the pot. Lower them gently with a spoon so they do not crack.
2. Pour in cold water until it is 2 cm above the eggs.
3. Put the pot on high heat and bring to a boil.
4. As soon as it boils, turn the heat off and put the lid on.
5. Start the timer for 11 minutes.
6. When the timer rings, pour out the hot water.
7. Fill the pot with cold water (add ice if you have it) and leave for 10 minutes.

PEEL AND FILL
8. Tap each egg on the counter and roll it gently to crack the shell all around.
9. Peel each egg under a little running water. The shell comes off easier.
10. Cut each egg in half lengthwise with a sharp knife.
11. Scoop the yolks into a bowl with a teaspoon.
12. Put the whites on the platter, hollow side up.
13. Mash the yolks with a fork until there are no lumps.
14. Add the mayonnaise, mustard, lime juice, salt and pepper.
15. Mix until smooth and creamy.
16. Spoon the yolk mixture back into the whites, about 1 heaped teaspoon in each half (24 halves).
17. Sprinkle the paprika over the top.
18. Cover the platter and keep it in the fridge for at least 20 minutes.
✅ READY WHEN: the filling is smooth and fluffy, piled up in each white, and the eggs are cold.
⚠️ A grey-green ring around the yolk means the eggs cooked too long. They are still safe to eat.
⚠️ Do not add more lime juice. Too much makes the filling runny and sour.
