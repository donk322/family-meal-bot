🍓 BERRIES WITH SOUR CREAM AND HONEY — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A very light dessert. It can also be served at breakfast.
⏱ 15 minutes. Start at T minus 20 min.

YOU NEED
• Berries (raspberries, strawberries, blueberries or a mix): 800 g
• Sour cream: 320 ml (cold)
• Honey: 80 g (about 4 tablespoons)
• Colander, large bowl, small bowl

STEPS
1. Put the berries in a colander and rinse under cold water for 30 seconds.
2. Let them drain for 5 minutes.
3. Cut the green tops off any strawberries. Cut large strawberries in half.
4. Put all the berries in a large bowl. Cover it and keep it in the fridge.
5. Put the cold sour cream in a small bowl. Stir it for 20 seconds until smooth.
6. Just before serving, put the sour cream on top of the berries in big spoonfuls.
7. Drizzle the honey over the sour cream.
8. Do not stir. Serve at once with a big spoon.
✅ READY WHEN: the berries are covered with sour cream and a drizzle of honey on top.
⚠️ Do not add the sour cream too early. The berries leak juice and the dessert turns watery.

🍮 BAKED BERRY CUSTARD — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A soft baked egg custard with raspberries. It can also be served at breakfast.
⏱ 65 minutes (oven). Start at T minus 70 min, or make it earlier and serve cold.

YOU NEED
• Eggs: 8
• Milk: 800 ml
• Honey: 100 g (5 tablespoons)
• Raspberries: 300 g
• Vanilla extract: 2 teaspoons
• Baking dish about 23 x 33 cm, a bigger deep tray for hot water, pot, whisk, jug, thermometer

GET READY
1. Turn the oven on to 160°C. Put the shelf in the middle.
2. Boil a kettle or a pot of water. You need about 1.5 litres.
3. Heat the milk in a pot on medium heat until small bubbles appear at the edge. Do not boil.
4. Crack the eggs into a big bowl. Add the honey and the vanilla.
5. Whisk for 1 minute until pale and smooth.
6. Pour the hot milk into the eggs in a thin stream, whisking all the time.
7. Pour the mixture through a sieve into a jug.
8. Spread the raspberries over the bottom of the baking dish.

BAKE
9. Put the baking dish inside the bigger tray on the oven shelf.
10. Pour the custard mixture over the raspberries.
11. Pour the hot water into the bigger tray, until it reaches halfway up the sides of the baking dish.
12. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes.
13. Shake the dish gently. The middle should still wobble a little, like jelly.
14. Push the thermometer into the middle. It must read at least 77°C.
15. Take the baking dish out of the hot water. Let it cool for 30 minutes.
✅ READY WHEN: the custard is set at the edges, wobbles slightly in the middle, and reads 77°C or more.
⚠️ Do not let the milk boil in step 3. It would cook the eggs in the bowl.
⚠️ The water bath is important. Without it the custard turns rubbery.

🥞 FLOUR-FREE CREPES WITH BERRIES — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2 crepes per portion. Thin crepes made with arrowroot instead of flour.
⏱ 60 minutes (large non-stick pan). Start at T minus 65 min.

YOU NEED
• Eggs: 8
• Milk: 400 ml
• Arrowroot: 130 g
• Butter: 30 g
• Berries: 400 g
• Sour cream: 200 ml
• Honey: 40 g (2 tablespoons)
• Vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon
• Salt: 1 pinch
• Big bowl, whisk, sieve, large non-stick pan (about 24 cm), ladle, spatula, foil

BATTER
1. Crack the eggs into a big bowl. Add the milk, the vanilla and the salt.
2. Whisk for 30 seconds.
3. Add the arrowroot a little at a time, whisking all the time.
4. Pour the batter through a sieve into a jug, to take out lumps.
5. Let it rest for 15 minutes.
6. Melt the butter and stir 2 teaspoons into the batter.

FILLING
7. Cut large strawberries in half. Wash and drain all the berries.
8. Mix the sour cream and the honey in a bowl.

COOK (16 crepes, one at a time)
9. Heat the pan on medium heat for 2 minutes. Rub it with a little melted butter.
10. Stir the batter. Pour in 1 ladle (about 70 ml) and tilt the pan so the batter covers the bottom.
11. Cook for 1 to 1 1/2 minutes, until the edges lift and the top looks dry.
12. Flip the crepe with a spatula. Cook for 30 seconds.
13. Move it to a plate and cover with foil. Stir the batter before each crepe.
14. Repeat steps 10 to 13 until all the batter is used.

FILL
15. Put 1 tablespoon of sour cream mix and some berries on each crepe.
16. Fold it in half, then in half again.
17. Put them on a platter.
✅ READY WHEN: the crepes are thin, pale gold, flexible and do not tear when folded.
⚠️ This is a flour-free batter. The crepes are thinner and a bit chewier than normal crepes. This is expected.
⚠️ Stir the batter before every crepe. The arrowroot sinks to the bottom.

🥜 PEANUT BUTTER AND HONEY BALLS — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
24 small balls, 3 per portion. Make them ahead.
⏱ 20 minutes of work and 30 minutes in the fridge. Start at T minus 60 min.

YOU NEED
• Peanut butter (smooth, natural): 300 g
• Honey: 100 g (5 tablespoons)
• Vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon
• Salt: 1 pinch
• Bowl, spoon, baking tray, baking paper, cling film

STEPS
1. Cover the baking tray with baking paper.
2. Put the peanut butter, the honey, the vanilla and the salt in a bowl.
3. Mix with a spoon for 1 to 2 minutes until smooth and thick.
4. Put the bowl in the fridge for 15 minutes so the mixture firms up.
5. Wet your hands with cold water.
6. Take 1 heaped tablespoon of the mixture and roll it into a ball.
7. Put the ball on the tray. Repeat to make 24 balls.
8. Cover the tray with cling film.
9. Put the tray in the fridge for 30 minutes.
10. Put the balls on a platter just before serving.
✅ READY WHEN: the balls are firm, hold their shape and are not sticky to the touch.
⚠️ If the mixture is too soft to roll, put it back in the fridge for 10 more minutes.
⚠️ Keep them cold. They get soft at room temperature after 30 minutes.

🍒 WARM CHERRIES WITH VANILLA AND SOUR CREAM — serves 8
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
It can also be served at breakfast.
⏱ 50 minutes, most of it is taking out the stones. Start at T minus 55 min.

YOU NEED
• Cherries: 1600 g
• Honey: 80 g (4 tablespoons)
• Vanilla extract: 2 teaspoons
• Arrowroot: 1 tablespoon
• Water: 100 ml
• Sour cream: 240 ml
• Medium pot, small bowl, sharp knife, 2 bowls (one for stones)

GET READY
1. Wash the cherries and remove the stalks.
2. Cut each cherry in half around the middle.
3. Remove the stone with the tip of the knife.
4. Put the halves in a bowl and the stones in another bowl.
5. Mix the arrowroot with 3 tablespoons of cold water in a cup until smooth.

COOK
6. Put the cherries, the honey and 100 ml of water in the pot.
7. Heat on medium heat for 3 minutes, until the juice starts to run.
8. Turn the heat to low. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring now and then.
9. Stir the arrowroot mix and pour it in.
10. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring, until the juice is thicker and shiny.
11. Take the pot off the heat. Stir in the vanilla.
12. Let it stand for 5 minutes.

SERVE
13. Spoon the warm cherries into a large bowl.
14. Put the cold sour cream in a small bowl next to it, with a spoon.
✅ READY WHEN: the cherries are soft but still hold their shape, and the juice is thick, dark red and shiny.
⚠️ Check the cherries again for stones before serving.
⚠️ Do not boil the cherries hard. They fall apart.
